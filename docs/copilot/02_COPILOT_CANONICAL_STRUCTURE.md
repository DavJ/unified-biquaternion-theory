# Copilot Instructions 02 — Canonical Theory Structure

The active GR chain is:

1. `Theta(q,tau)` — fundamental biquaternionic field.
2. A stated covariant representation, generically two-sided:
   `D_mu Theta=partial_mu Theta+A_mu Theta-Theta B_mu`.
3. `E_mu=N0^(-1/2)D_mu Theta` — UBT tetrad.
4. Central anticommutator — Lorentz metric.
5. `omega(e,T)=omega_LC(e)+K(T)` — unique compatible frame connection once
   tetrad and torsion are specified.
6. `[D_mu,D_nu]Theta=F^A Theta-Theta F^B` — curvature identity.
7. The pure Lorentz pair reduces to `A_mu=Omega_mu`, `B_mu=-Omega_mu^ddagger` but is a concurrent-vector no-go for generic curved GR and Schwarzschild with nonzero mass.
8. The remaining two-sided route must derive a nontrivial relative action as composite or auxiliary and prove that it is nonpropagating.
9. Prescribed curved integrability has an exact augmented-holonomy criterion; self-consistent curved integrability and fundamental torsion dynamics remain narrowed at full-theory level.
10. Minimal Palatini torsion elimination and the Einstein--Lambda/Lovelock endpoint are conditional branches, not an unconditional action derivation.
11. Einstein dynamics from the same canonical action — open.

The constant-tetrad/Minkowski branch has an explicit affine `Theta`
representer. The one-sided invertible torsion-free curved branch is a proved
conditional no-go. The pure compatible two-sided pair is also a proved no-go
for generic curved GR; only a derived nontrivial relative two-sided action
remains structurally viable, and it is not yet an existence theorem.

Projection/fiber/embedding routes are noncanonical historical or exploratory
material.
