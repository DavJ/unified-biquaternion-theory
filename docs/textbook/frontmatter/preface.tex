\chapter*{Preface (Read Me First)}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

This book is the working learning and reference text for the Unified Biquaternion Theory
programme.  Its first job is to make the mathematics understandable enough that the
connections between UBT branches can be reconstructed later without relying on memory.
For that reason, classical mathematics is intentionally included when it is needed for
the UBT big picture, even when the underlying theorem is old and well known.

The book distinguishes three layers rather than discarding the first one:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{classical foundations}: standard mathematics and physics, with enough
        derivation to make the text self-contained;
  \item \textbf{UBT bridges}: controlled mappings from those standard structures into
        UBT notation or reduced models;
  \item \textbf{UBT claims and open gaps}: statements that depend on the canonical UBT
        field, action, dynamics, or empirical programme.
\end{enumerate}

A classical identity does not become novel because UBT uses it.  Conversely, the fact
that an identity is classical is not a reason to omit it: the research value may lie in
how several established structures are assembled and which new UBT question their
combination makes precise.  Canonical claim documents remain conservative; this textbook
is allowed to be expansive and didactic as long as status labels are explicit.

The textbook must not import active theory from \texttt{ARCHIVE/}.  Current status is
read first from the Tier-A ledgers (especially \texttt{STATUS.md},
\texttt{WHAT\_IS\_PROVED.md}, and \texttt{CLAIMS.yaml}); active machine-verified
mathematics may then be cited from \texttt{canonical/}.  Standard mathematics may be
re-derived directly in the textbook.  Historical files are used only when the history of
an idea is itself being discussed.  The migration table is maintained in
\texttt{docs/textbook/SOURCE\_MAP.md}.

Every live source file also follows the repository's machine-readable AI-provenance
policy.  This book is Tier C working material unless the human editor explicitly changes
the tier in the authoritative provenance map; an AI agent does not promote or attest it.
