# UBT Scope and Claim Levels

This document defines repository scope boundaries and claim-level discipline.

---

## Repository Scope Layers

- `canonical/`: authoritative mathematical/physical core
- `research_tracks/`: active scientific work not fully closed
- `speculative_extensions/`: philosophical/metaphysical exploratory hypotheses
- `ARCHIVE/`: historical and superseded material

---

## Claim Levels

### CANONICAL_THEOREM
- fully specified assumptions
- proof or derivation included
- belongs in `canonical/`

### DERIVED_WITH_ASSUMPTIONS
- mathematically derived under stated assumptions
- assumptions and gaps explicit

### NUMERICAL_EVIDENCE
- supported by reproducible scripts/data
- not a proof

### CONJECTURE
- plausible research direction
- not proven
- belongs in `research_tracks/`

### OPEN_GAP
- explicitly unresolved issue
- should not be hidden

### SPECULATIVE_INTERPRETATION
- philosophical or metaphysical interpretation
- belongs only in `speculative_extensions/`
- must not be presented as established physics

---

## Mandatory Repository Rule

No speculative or metaphysical claim may appear in `canonical/` or in the
abstract/introduction/conclusion of physics papers unless it is explicitly
marked as speculative and separated from the mathematical derivation.

---

## Canonical Boundary Reminder

Claims involving consciousness, psychons, afterlife/survival-of-consciousness,
ThetaComm, soul/immortality, and Matrix/simulation ontology are non-canonical
unless elevated by independent reproducible empirical protocol and explicit
status update in `CLAIMS_MATRIX.md`.
