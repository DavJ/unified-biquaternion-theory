\begin{abstract}
This document records the minimal active UBT axioms and separates them from
standard geometric consequences and open dynamical bridges.  In particular,
the classical metric is the central anticommutator of a covariant
biquaternionic tetrad; it is not a real-part, trace, phase, or fiber projection.
\end{abstract}

\section{Structural axioms}

\begin{axiom}[Biquaternion algebra]
The algebraic arena is
\[
 \mathbb B=\mathbb C\otimes\mathbb H\cong\operatorname{Mat}(2,\mathbb C),
\]
with a fixed quaternion conjugation $\sharp$ and the commuting complex unit
$i$.
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}[Fundamental field and complex time]
The fundamental field is
\[
 \Theta(q,\tau)\in\mathbb B,
 \qquad \tau=t+i\psi.
\]
The exact intrinsic regularity class in $q$ and the boundary/periodicity
conditions in $\psi$ remain part of the canonical regularity programme.  No
preferred $\psi$ section or compact-fiber average defines the metric.
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}[Covariant tetrad and central metric]
Define
\[
 E_\mu:=\mathcal N_0^{-1/2}D_\mu\Theta,
 \qquad \mathcal N_0>0\text{ constant}.
\]
The classical Lorentz sector is the real slice
\[
 E_\mu=i e_\mu{}^0\mathbf1+e_\mu{}^k\mathbf e_k,
 \qquad e_\mu{}^a\in\mathbb R,
\]
and the metric is defined by the full central identity
\[
 \boxed{\frac12(E_\mu^\sharp E_\nu+E_\nu^\sharp E_\mu)
       =g_{\mu\nu}\mathbf1.}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
 g_{\mu\nu}=e_\mu{}^a e_\nu{}^b\eta_{ab},
 \qquad \eta=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1).
\]
No trace, componentwise real part, phase projector, preferred section,
embedding map, or compact-$\psi$ average is part of this definition.
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}[Covariant representation]
The operator $D_\mu$ must be specified together with its representation and
multiplication side.  The generic algebra-native curved candidate is the
bimodule derivative
\[
 D_\mu\Theta=\partial_\mu\Theta+A_\mu\Theta-\Theta B_\mu.
\]
A one-sided derivative may be used only in a sector where its assumptions and
curvature restrictions are explicitly stated.
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}[Action principle]
The complete dynamics must follow from one explicitly specified UBT action and
its variational principle.  The action must determine the $\Theta$ equation,
the left/right connection representation, and the torsion equation or
constraint.  The insertion of an Einstein--Hilbert term composed with the
emergent metric may be studied as a conditional branch but is not by itself a
derivation of Einstein dynamics from a deeper UBT invariant.
\end{axiom}

\section{Standard consequences and current theorem status}

For every nondegenerate tetrad, the map $e\mapsto e\eta e^{\mathsf T}$ has
rank ten and a six-dimensional local-Lorentz kernel.  For every specified
torsion and tetrad, metric compatibility uniquely reconstructs
\[
 \omega=\mathring\omega(e)+K(T),
 \qquad K_{abc}=\tfrac12(T_{cab}-T_{abc}-T_{bca}),
\]
using $T^a=de^a+\omega^a{}_b\wedge e^b$.  The torsion-free GR branch therefore
has the Levi--Civita spin connection.  These are theorems, not extra
independent axioms.

Every constant Lorentz tetrad has an affine representer
\[
 \Theta_{\rm aff}=\Theta_0+\sqrt{\mathcal N_0}\,\bar E_\mu x^\mu.
\]
The generic one-sided, invertible, torsion-free curved route obeys a conditional
flatness no-go.  The two-sided derivative instead has
\[
 [D_\mu,D_\nu]\Theta=F^A_{\mu\nu}\Theta-\Theta F^B_{\mu\nu},
\]
which permits nonzero intertwined curvatures but does not prove curved-space
existence.
