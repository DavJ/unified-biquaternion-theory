\section{Geometric variables}
The single fundamental field is $\Theta(q,\tau)$.  Its covariant first jet is
\[
 E_\mu=\mathcal N_0^{-1/2}D_\mu\Theta,
\]
and the classical metric is the central anticommutator
\[
 \frac12(E_\mu^\sharp E_\nu+E_\nu^\sharp E_\mu)=g_{\mu\nu}\mathbf1.
\]
For a nondegenerate tetrad and specified torsion, the metric-compatible frame
connection is reconstructed kinematically as
$\omega=\mathring\omega(e)+K(T)$.  Lorentz-slice and metric compatibility
reduce the pure no-new-field bimodule representative to
$A_\mu=\Omega_\mu$, $B_\mu=-\Omega_\mu^\ddagger$, modulo a common central term
that cancels.  With $K=0$ that representative is a proved no-go for the
non-flat Schwarzschild vacuum exterior because a generated tetrad then implies
$\mathring\nabla_\mu V^\nu=\delta_\mu{}^\nu$.  The no-go is torsion-free.
Conversely, an explicit composite metric-compatible contortion gives a local
single-$\Theta$ representer for every smooth Lorentzian tetrad on a non-null
Gaussian patch.  Therefore the action-level unknown is not arbitrary extra
connection fields: it is whether canonical UBT selects that torsionful
composite branch, or an optional torsion-free relative bimodule action that is
composite or auxiliary, together with the physical and global conditions.

\section{Internal action and master equation}
A kinetic/potential action of the form
\[
 S_{\Theta}=\frac12\int\sqrt{-g}\,\langle D_\mu\Theta,D^\mu\Theta\rangle
 -\kappa V[\Theta]
\]
can yield a master equation $D^\dagger D\Theta=\kappa\mathcal T$ when the source
is variationally integrable.  On the Lorentz slice, full Lorentz invariance of
a real symmetric nondegenerate kinetic pairing selects the $\sharp$/Minkowski
form uniquely up to an overall scale; that scale and sign remain dynamical
inputs.  This establishes compatibility of the internal master equation with
an action, not the origin of the complete gravitational action.

\section{Minimal first-order conditional branch}
Consider the standard Hilbert--Palatini/Einstein--Cartan effective branch
\[
 S_{\rm HP}[e,\omega]
 =\frac1{4\kappa}\int\epsilon_{abcd}e^a\wedge e^b\wedge R^{cd}(\omega)
 -\frac{\Lambda}{24\kappa}\int\epsilon_{abcd}e^a\wedge e^b\wedge e^c\wedge e^d
 +S_{\rm m}[e,\omega,\Theta].
\]
Independent variation of the Lorentz connection gives the algebraic Cartan
equation
\[
 \epsilon_{abcd}T^a\wedge e^b=\kappa\tau_{cd}.
\]
For a nondegenerate tetrad its $24\times24$ torsion map is pointwise
invertible.  Hence zero spin current implies $T=0$ and a specified spin current
implies a unique contorsion.  This closes the algebraic torsion-selection
subproblem inside the minimal branch.  The direct fixed-background
$\Theta$ matter current is derived in the canonical action audit, but neither
the minimal branch itself nor the full composite $\Theta$-only variation is
derived from fundamental UBT.

With zero spin current the tetrad equation becomes
\[
 G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=\kappa T_{\mu\nu}.
\]
Moreover, if the macroscopic metric equation satisfies the ordinary
four-dimensional Lovelock hypotheses (locality, diffeomorphism covariance,
divergence freedom, second-order metric dynamics, and no additional light
geometric fields), its gravitational tensor is necessarily
$aG_{\mu\nu}+bg_{\mu\nu}$.  Thus Einstein--$\Lambda$ is the unique conditional
infrared endpoint under these assumptions.

\section{Curved integrability and symmetry conditions}
For prescribed $E_\mu,A_\mu,B_\mu$, the two-sided defining equation
\[
 \partial_\mu\Theta+A_\mu\Theta-\Theta B_\mu
 =\sqrt{\mathcal N_0}E_\mu
\]
can be written as parallel transport of the augmented vector
$Y=(\Theta,1)^T$.  A single-valued solution with initial value $Y_0$ exists
exactly when the augmented holonomy fixes $Y_0$; flat augmented curvature is a
sufficient condition on a simply connected domain.  The remaining curved gap is the self-consistent action-level
selection of $(E,A,B,T)$, regularity and global continuation.  The pure
Lorentz representative $A=\Omega$, $B=-\Omega^\ddagger$ is excluded only in
the torsion-free generated-tetrad branch by the concurrent-vector theorem.
Composite contortion supplies a local curved right inverse inside the same
one-connection pairing.  A common central constant or one-form cannot evade
the torsion-free obstruction because it cancels from the bimodule derivative.

The Lorentz slice is the fixed set of the anti-linear involution
$\mathcal JX=-\overline{X^\sharp}$.  A unique $\mathcal J$-equivariant Cauchy
evolution with fixed sources and data preserves this slice.  Likewise, a
$\psi$-flow tangent to a local Lorentz gauge orbit leaves $g_{\mu\nu}$ exactly
unchanged, while unique $\psi$-translation-invariant evolution preserves
strictly $\psi$-independent data.  These are sufficient symmetry-propagation
criteria, not yet a proof that the final canonical action satisfies them.

\section{Variation warning}
A fresh variation of any composed or effective branch must include
\[
 \delta E_\mu=\mathcal N_0^{-1/2}
 \left(D_\mu\delta\Theta+\delta A_\mu\Theta-\Theta\delta B_\mu\right),
\]
together with variations induced by $e$, torsion and the connection.  A
conditional Palatini/Lovelock endpoint must never be relabelled as a derivation
of the full canonical UBT action.
