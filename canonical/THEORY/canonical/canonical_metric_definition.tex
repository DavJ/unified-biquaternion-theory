\section{Definition}
The unique fundamental field remains $\Theta(q,\tau)\in\mathbb B$.  Define its
covariant tetrad
\[
 E_\mu=\mathcal N_0^{-1/2}D_\mu\Theta,
 \qquad \mathcal N_0>0\text{ constant}.
\]
In the classical Lorentz sector,
\[
 E_\mu=i e_\mu{}^0\mathbf1+e_\mu{}^k\mathbf e_k,
 \qquad e_\mu{}^a\in\mathbb R.
\]
With quaternion conjugation $\sharp$,
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\frac12(E_\mu^\sharp E_\nu+E_\nu^\sharp E_\mu)
       =g_{\mu\nu}\mathbf1.}
\end{equation}
The metric is the central coefficient of the full identity; no trace,
real-part operation, phase projection, or compact-fiber average is used.

\section{Lorentz signature}
Direct multiplication gives
\[
 g_{\mu\nu}=e_\mu{}^a e_\nu{}^b\eta_{ab},
 \qquad \eta=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1).
\]
The time sign follows from the commuting complex coefficient $i^2=-1$.
Nondegeneracy is $\det(e_\mu{}^a)\ne0$.

\section{Rank}
At a nondegenerate tetrad the differential of $e\mapsto e\eta e^{\mathsf T}$
has rank ten.  For any symmetric $h_{\mu\nu}$,
\[
 \delta e_\mu{}^a=\frac12h_{\mu\rho}e^{\rho a}
\]
gives $\delta g_{\mu\nu}=h_{\mu\nu}$.  The six-dimensional kernel is the
local Lorentz algebra.  Thus the local kinematic rank problem is solved without
a fiber completion.

\section{Connection and remaining dynamical bridge}
For a nondegenerate tetrad and specified torsion, the metric-compatible frame
connection is uniquely
\[
 \omega=\mathring\omega(e)+K(T),
 \qquad K_{abc}=\tfrac12(T_{cab}-T_{abc}-T_{bca}),
\]
for $T^a=de^a+\omega^a{}_b\wedge e^b$. Hence the torsion-free classical GR
branch has the unique Levi--Civita spin connection. This closes the kinematic
connection ambiguity, not the UBT dynamics selecting torsion or the exact
representation acting on $\Theta$.

Every constant Lorentz tetrad has the flat affine representer
\[
 \Theta_{\rm aff}=\Theta_0+\sqrt{\mathcal N_0}\,\bar E_\mu x^\mu.
\]
For curved geometry the active algebra-native candidate is two-sided,
\[
 D_\mu\Theta=\partial_\mu\Theta+A_\mu\Theta-\Theta B_\mu.
\]
A naive one-sided invertible torsion-free branch forces zero curvature under
the stated compatibility assumptions. The two-sided identity avoids this
specific obstruction, but curved local/global existence, torsion dynamics,
Lorentz-slice preservation by the full equations, and Einstein dynamics remain
open. Compact-$\psi$ fiber averaging remains noncanonical.
