\begin{abstract}
This document separates the compact UBT master-equation notation from the
proved covariant-tetrad kinematics and from the still-open Einstein/action
bridge.  It does not describe GR as a real projection of a biquaternionic
Einstein equation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Master-equation notation}

A compact form used in UBT is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:master}
 D^\dagger D\Theta=\kappa\mathcal T.
\end{equation}
Its exact source functional, regularity domain, left/right connection
representation, and common-action origin must be specified in each derivation.
Equation~\eqref{eq:master} is therefore not, by notation alone, a completed
derivation of the gravitational field equations.

\section{Projection-free metric kinematics}

\[
 E_\mu=\mathcal N_0^{-1/2}D_\mu\Theta,
 \qquad
 \frac12(E_\mu^\sharp E_\nu+E_\nu^\sharp E_\mu)
 =g_{\mu\nu}\mathbf1.
\]
The nondegenerate tetrad-to-metric differential has rank ten.  For specified
torsion, the compatible frame connection is uniquely
\[
 \omega=\mathring\omega(e)+K(T),
 \qquad K_{abc}=\tfrac12(T_{cab}-T_{abc}-T_{bca}).
\]
The torsion-free branch is the standard Levi--Civita spin connection.

Every constant Lorentz tetrad has an affine single-$\Theta$ representer.  For
curved geometry the general algebra-native candidate is
\[
 D_\mu\Theta=\partial_\mu\Theta+A_\mu\Theta-\Theta B_\mu,
 \qquad
 [D_\mu,D_\nu]\Theta=F^A_{\mu\nu}\Theta-\Theta F^B_{\mu\nu}.
\]
The no-new-field Lorentz representative is uniquely
$A_\mu=\Omega_\mu$, $B_\mu=-\Omega_\mu^\ddagger$ modulo a cancelling central
term.  In the torsion-free generated-tetrad branch it implies a concurrent
vector $\mathring\nabla_\mu V^\nu=\delta_\mu{}^\nu$, so it excludes the
non-flat Schwarzschild vacuum exterior with nonzero mass.  This is not a no-go
for the same pair with composite torsion: a local metric-compatible contortion
construction gives a single-$\Theta$ representer for every smooth tetrad.
Canonical dynamics must select and physically constrain that torsionful branch,
or else derive an optional torsion-free relative left/right action as a
composite or auxiliary nonpropagating variable.

\section{Einstein bridge}

The target equation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:einstein}
 G_{\mu\nu}[g]=\kappa T_{\mu\nu}
\end{equation}
has not yet been derived unconditionally from the same canonical UBT action
that determines $\Theta$, the selected torsion/relative representation, and its physical couplings.  A composed
Einstein--Hilbert branch can reproduce standard tetrad GR under its assumptions,
but it does not close GAP-10D or GAP-B-MASTER.

When a matter action is specified,
\[
 T_{\mu\nu}=-\frac{2}{\sqrt{-g}}
 \frac{\delta(\sqrt{-g}\,\mathcal L_{\rm matter})}{\delta g^{\mu\nu}}.
\]
Claims about conservation or positivity must be tied to that complete action
and its equations.

\section{Gauge and matter equations}

Maxwell, Dirac, and Yang--Mills equations may appear in appropriate
representation/limit sectors of a specified UBT action.  Standard equations
must not be relabelled as UBT derivations unless the embedding, normalization,
field content, and variational reduction are shown.  Their current detailed
statuses are maintained in `CLAIMS.yaml` and the relevant canonical bridge
files.
