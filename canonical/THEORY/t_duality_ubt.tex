\section{Setup}
On the compact phase circle \(S^1_\psi\) with radius \(R\), consider the UBT
effective quadratic action model
\[
S[\Theta,R]=\int\!\left[\frac{(\partial_\mu\Theta)^2}{R}+R(\partial_\psi\Theta)^2\right]\mathrm{d}^4x\,\mathrm{d}\psi.
\]
Under \(R\mapsto 1/R\), momentum and winding labels are exchanged in the usual
KK/T-duality pattern.

\section{Lemma target}
\textbf{Lemma target.} There exists a dual field map \(\Theta\mapsto\widetilde\Theta\)
such that
\[
S[\Theta,R]=S[\widetilde\Theta,1/R].
\]
If this is established in the canonical UBT action, then \(R=1\) (Planck units)
is the self-dual point and corresponds to \(\tau=i\) in the modular parameter
normalization used in the alpha track.

\section{Impact on Gap G137-B}
If T-duality symmetry is proved at [L1], then \(\tau=i\) is canonically selected
as the self-dual point for the modular \(B\)-candidate chain. This would tighten
the interpretation of \(B(\tau=i)\), but does not by itself derive \(\alpha\).
