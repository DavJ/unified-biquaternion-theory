The metric is generated by the covariant first jet
\[
 E_\mu=\mathcal N_0^{-1/2}D_\mu\Theta,
 \qquad
 \frac12(E_\mu^\sharp E_\nu+E_\nu^\sharp E_\mu)

not by the value of $\Theta$ alone.  The local tetrad-to-metric map has rank
ten; its six-dimensional kernel is local Lorentz freedom.  The former
single-value rank obstruction and compact-fiber repair are not part of the
minimal canonical route.

\[
 E_\mu=\mathcal N_0^{-1/2}
 \left[\partial_\mu\Theta+A_\mu[E,T]\Theta-\Theta B_\mu[E,T]\right].
\]
The special choice $A=\Omega$, $B=-\Omega^\ddagger$ is a diagnostic no-go
only in the torsion-free generated-tetrad branch.  Composite contortion gives
an explicit local curved right inverse inside the same one-connection pairing.
The unresolved object is now dynamical selection and physical admissibility of
that torsion, or alternatively an optional torsion-free relative bimodule
completion.
It is an implicit nonlinear first-order PDE/fixed-point system.  If the allowed
field class contains Jacobi theta functions, the complete system may also be
transcendental; that is a distinct property.

The former compact-$\psi$ fiber-average construction is retained only as an
exploratory completion and must not be cited as the canonical metric or the
primary resolution of GAP-10.
