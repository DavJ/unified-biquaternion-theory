\section{Decision question}
This audit asks whether the local four-dimensional infrared GR-recovery status
can be upgraded from \texttt{CLOSED\_CONDITIONALLY} to \texttt{CLOSED} using
the current canonical axioms and proved repository results.  It tests the
microscopic single-$\Theta$ selector, the Einstein--Hilbert coefficient and
measure, the composite Hessian, physical $\psi$-sector stability, and the
logical relevance of global patching to a local claim.

% BILINGUAL-UNIT: unconditional.selector
\section{Exact selector obstruction}
On the Lorentz-real branch let
\begin{equation}
 E_\mu=\mathcal N_0^{-1/2}D_\mu\Theta,
 \qquad \langle E_\mu,E_\nu\rangle_\sharp=g_{\mu\nu}.
\end{equation}
Then the most direct covariant quadratic first-jet candidate obeys
\begin{equation}\label{eq:collapse}
 g^{\mu\nu}\langle D_\mu\Theta,D_\nu\Theta\rangle_\sharp
 =\mathcal N_0 g^{\mu\nu}g_{\mu\nu}=4\mathcal N_0.
\end{equation}
Thus its action is a cosmological volume functional.  It is not an independent
kinetic selector and cannot derive the Einstein--Hilbert term.

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional closure is not derivable from the current inputs]
Let $\mathcal K$ be the current set of kinematic inputs: the covariant-tetrad
definition, central metric identity, local Lorentz and diffeomorphism
symmetries, connection reconstruction, and local split-jet right inverse.
For every real $c$ the functional
\begin{equation}\label{eq:family}
 S_c[\Theta]=S_0[\Theta]+c\int d^4x\sqrt{-g[\Theta]}
 R[g[\Theta]]
\end{equation}
has the same inputs $\mathcal K$.  Different $c$ give different Newton
coefficients, including $c=0$.  Consequently $\mathcal K$ neither selects the
Einstein--Hilbert term nor fixes a finite positive coefficient.  Equation
\eqref{eq:collapse} does not remove this freedom.  Therefore the assumptions
of the conditional recovery theorem are not consequences of the presently
finalized microscopic single-$\Theta$ theory.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every member of Eq.~\eqref{eq:family} has the same field-to-tetrad map and the
same kinematic identities.  The added term is separately diffeomorphism and
local-Lorentz invariant for every $c$, while its metric variation and Newton
coefficient depend on $c$.  Hence the common inputs cannot imply one value of
$c$ or exclude $c=0$.  Equation~\eqref{eq:collapse} shows that the displayed
quadratic first-jet candidate adds only a volume term, so it supplies no
missing selection premise.
\end{proof}

% BILINGUAL-UNIT: unconditional.hessian
\section{Hessian, measure, and $\psi$ sector}
The induced-gravity calculation is exact only after assuming a gauge-fixed
Laplace-type physical Hessian, mode count $N_B$, curvature coupling $\xi$,
cutoff identification, regulator, and constrained fluctuation measure.  The
fixed-background principal symbol does not equal the full composite Hessian:
varying $\Theta$ also varies $E[\Theta]$, $g[\Theta]$, and the reconstructed
connection.  None of the current theorems derives those missing data.

Likewise, a four-dimensional zero-mode truncation is physically selected only
if the finalized microscopic evolution preserves it and unwanted $\psi$ modes
are stable or decouple.  The existing symmetry-propagation theorem is
conditional on equivariance and unique evolution; those hypotheses have not
been proved for a finalized microscopic action.  Hence $\psi$ stability is
not needed to vary an already assumed four-dimensional effective action, but
it is needed to derive that physical branch unconditionally from UBT.

% BILINGUAL-UNIT: unconditional.global
\section{Local versus global scope}
Global, horizon, null-patch, and topological continuation is not a premise of
the stated \emph{local} GR-recovery theorem.  It therefore cannot block a
local status upgrade by itself.  It remains necessary for a global
Schwarzschild representative or a claim covering all GR spacetimes.  This
scope separation does not cure the local action-selection obstruction above.

\section{Verdict}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=yellow!7!white,colframe=yellow!55!black,title=Status decision]
\textbf{Do not upgrade the status.}  The strongest justified verdict remains
\textbf{GR recovery: CLOSED CONDITIONALLY} for local four-dimensional
infrared Einstein gravity.  Unconditional \texttt{CLOSED} requires a derived
microscopic selector that fixes a finite positive Einstein--Hilbert
coefficient and a controlled composite Hessian/measure, plus physical
$\psi$-sector selection.  Global patching is outside this local decision.
\end{tcolorbox}
