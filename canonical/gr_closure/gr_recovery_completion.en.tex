\section{Scope}
This note closes the \emph{GR-recovery} question at the level already supported
by the repository: four-dimensional local infrared Einstein gravity under the
explicit effective-action assumptions documented in
\texttt{gap\_10\_gr\_effective\_completion.tex}.  It does not claim a complete
microscopic single-$\Theta$ derivation of that effective action or a
first-principles numerical prediction of Newton's constant.

% BILINGUAL-UNIT: recovery.inputs
\section{Inputs already established}
We use four existing results.
\begin{enumerate}
\item On every regular non-null local patch, the split-jet map has an explicit
right inverse for every smooth tetrad while the physical spacetime connection
remains Levi--Civita.
\item The split-jet multiplier action has vanishing multiplier on shell; its
jet variables are algebraic, nonpropagating, and contribute no on-shell stress
or physical spin source.
\item Under the stated spectral/Hessian assumptions, the two-derivative
infrared effective action is Einstein--$\Lambda$ with a finite renormalized
coefficient $G_{\rm R}$ (or the conditional induced coefficient
$G_{\rm ind}$).
\item The standard Regge--Wheeler and Zerilli equations are reductions of the
linearized Einstein system about Schwarzschild.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Recovery theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Conditional effective GR recovery]
Assume the infrared effective action on a regular non-null patch is
\begin{equation}
\Gamma_{\rm IR}[e,\text{aux}]
=-\frac{1}{16\pi G_{\rm R}}\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\,(R-2\Lambda_{\rm R})
+S_{\rm aux}+S_{\rm matter}+O(\nabla^4/\Lambda^2),
\end{equation}
with $0<G_{\rm R}<\infty$, and let $S_{\rm aux}$ be the proved split-jet
multiplier action.  Then, through two derivatives:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the auxiliary Euler--Lagrange equations enforce the split-jet relation
and imply that the multiplier vanishes on shell;
\item all auxiliary contributions to the tetrad and physical-connection field
equations vanish on shell;
\item the physical connection is Levi--Civita;
\item the remaining metric equation is
\begin{equation}
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda_{\rm R}g_{\mu\nu}
=8\pi G_{\rm R}T_{\mu\nu};
\end{equation}
\item because the split-jet map is a local right inverse for every tetrad,
every local solution of this Einstein equation admits an effective UBT
representative without adding a propagating gravitational degree of freedom.
\end{enumerate}
Therefore local four-dimensional GR recovery is closed conditionally at the
infrared effective level.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Items 1--2 are exactly the split-jet auxiliary theorem.  With the auxiliary
on-shell sources equal to zero, variation of the remaining two-derivative
functional is the standard Einstein--Hilbert variation, giving item 4 with the
Levi--Civita connection of item 3.  The split-jet right-inverse theorem then
lifts every resulting smooth local tetrad to the effective UBT variables,
proving item 5.  No value of $G_{\rm R}$ is fitted or derived in this argument;
only its finite positive renormalized existence is required for GR recovery.
\end{proof}

\section{Schwarzschild and perturbative corollaries}
\begin{corollary}[Schwarzschild recovery]
For $\Lambda_{\rm R}=0$ and vacuum matter, the complete Schwarzschild tetrad,
including its lapse, is an Einstein solution.  Hence every regular local
Schwarzschild patch has a split-jet effective UBT representative.  This closes
\texttt{GAP-U2Theta} \emph{for conditional GR recovery}.  It does not restore
the invalid historical single-$\Theta$ Schwarzschild ansatz; that ansatz
remains superseded by the canonical correction.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[Linear graviton recovery]
Linearization of the same recovered effective branch about Schwarzschild is the
standard linearized Einstein system.  Its odd- and even-parity gauge-invariant
reductions are the Regge--Wheeler and Zerilli equations already documented in
the repository.  Thus \texttt{GAP-B-MASTER} is closed for perturbations of the
recovered effective GR branch, while direct derivation of the same operator
from the microscopic master equation remains a stronger nonblocking
fundamental-completion problem.
\end{corollary}
