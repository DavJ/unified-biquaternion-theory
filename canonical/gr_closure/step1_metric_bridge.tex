\section{Step 1: Metric from the covariant biquaternionic tetrad}
The canonical result is proved in
\texttt{canonical/gr\_closure/covariant\_tetrad\_rank\_theorem.tex}.
Define $E_\mu=\mathcal N_0^{-1/2}D_\mu\Theta$ in the Lorentz slice.  Then
\[
 \frac12(E_\mu^\sharp E_\nu+E_\nu^\sharp E_\mu)
 =g_{\mu\nu}\mathbf1,
\]
so the metric is central without projection.  The tetrad-to-metric map has rank
ten at every nondegenerate tetrad.
