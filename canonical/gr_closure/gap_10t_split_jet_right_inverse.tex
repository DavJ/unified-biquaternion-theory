\begin{abstract}
The minimal one-connection architecture cannot simultaneously provide universal
single-$\Theta$ representability and exact generic torsion-free GR.  This note
constructs the smallest local kinematic completion after that no-go.  Physical
curvature and matter transport use the Levi--Civita connection of an arbitrary
prescribed tetrad.  Only the defining $\Theta$ jet is augmented by an explicitly
composite Lorentz contortion and a composite real dilation one-form.  For every
non-null Lorentz-real field $X$ and every smooth tetrad $E$, the formulas below
give $\widehat D_\mu X=\sqrt{\mathcal N_0}E_\mu$ identically.  The added jet
objects are algebraic in $(E,X,\mathring D X)$ and introduce no independent
propagating field.  The construction closes local exact-GR representability of
the split-connection architecture.  It does not derive the tetrad from
$\Theta$, select the jet representative from an action, or derive the
Hilbert--Palatini coefficient.
\end{abstract}

\section{Physical and jet connections}
Let $E_\mu=e_\mu{}^a\mathbf u_a$ be an arbitrary smooth nondegenerate Lorentz
tetrad and let $\mathring\omega(E)$ be its Levi--Civita spin connection.  This
is the \emph{physical} connection:
\begin{equation}
 \Omega_{\rm phys}=\mathring\Omega(E),\qquad T_{\rm phys}=0,
 \qquad R_{\rm phys}=R[\mathring\Omega(E)].
 \label{eq:physical}
\end{equation}
Therefore $R_{\rm phys}$ is as general as the Riemann curvature of the
prescribed GR tetrad.

Let $X=X^a\mathbf u_a$ be the Lorentz-real projection of $\Theta$ on a patch
where
\begin{equation}
 X^2:=\eta_{ab}X^aX^b\ne0,
 \qquad s:=\sqrt{\mathcal N_0}.
\end{equation}
Define the covariant mismatch
\begin{equation}
 Z_\mu{}^a:=s e_\mu{}^a-\mathring D_\mu X^a.
 \label{eq:Z}
\end{equation}
Decompose it into its component parallel to $X$ and its orthogonal component:
\begin{equation}
 w_\mu:=\frac{X_aZ_\mu{}^a}{X^2},
 \qquad
 Z_{\mu\perp}{}^a:=Z_\mu{}^a-w_\mu X^a,
 \qquad X_aZ_{\mu\perp}{}^a=0.
 \label{eq:decomp}
\end{equation}

\section{Exact algebraic right inverse}
\begin{theorem}[Split-jet right inverse]
Define the Lorentz-algebra-valued composite tensor
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 K^J_{\mu ab}:=
 \frac{Z_{\mu\perp a}X_b-X_aZ_{\mu\perp b}}{X^2},
 \qquad K^J_{\mu ab}=-K^J_{\mu ba}.}
 \label{eq:KJ}
\end{equation}
Let the jet derivative on $X$ be
\begin{equation}
 \widehat D_\mu X^a
 :=\mathring D_\mu X^a+K^{J\,a}{}_{\mu b}X^b+w_\mu X^a.
 \label{eq:Dhat}
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\widehat D_\mu X^a=s e_\mu{}^a}
 \label{eq:rightinverse}
\end{equation}
holds identically for every $(E,X)$ on the non-null patch.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:decomp} makes $Z_{\mu\perp}$ orthogonal to $X$.
Contracting Eq.~\eqref{eq:KJ} with $X^b$ gives
\begin{equation}
 K^{J\,a}{}_{\mu b}X^b
 =Z_{\mu\perp}{}^a.
\end{equation}
Thus
\begin{equation}
 \widehat D_\mu X^a
 =\mathring D_\mu X^a+Z_{\mu\perp}{}^a+w_\mu X^a
 =\mathring D_\mu X^a+Z_\mu{}^a
 =s e_\mu{}^a.
\end{equation}
\end{proof}

The general Lorentz solution differs from Eq.~\eqref{eq:KJ} by a stabilizer
term $C_{\mu ab}$ satisfying $C^a{}_{\mu b}X^b=0$.  Such a term is invisible in
$\widehat D X$; Eq.~\eqref{eq:KJ} is the covariant representative with no
stabilizer component.

\section{Biquaternionic bimodule form}
Let $\mathcal K^J_\mu$ be the spin lift of $K^J_{\mu ab}$.  The real one-form
$w_\mu$ can be implemented by a relative central action:
\begin{equation}
 A^J_\mu=\mathring\Omega_\mu+\mathcal K^J_\mu+\frac12w_\mu\mathbf1,
 \qquad
 B^J_\mu=-\mathring\Omega_\mu^{\dd}-\mathcal K_\mu^{J\dd}
          -\frac12w_\mu\mathbf1.
 \label{eq:AB}
\end{equation}
Then $\partial_\mu X+A^J_\mu X-XB^J_\mu$ is precisely
Eq.~\eqref{eq:Dhat}.  The relative central term is not a second physical gauge
field: it is the fixed composite scalar in Eq.~\eqref{eq:decomp} and acts only
in the defining jet.  It preserves the Lorentz-real slice but is not required
to preserve the Lorentz norm under jet transport.  Physical metric
compatibility remains entirely with Eq.~\eqref{eq:physical}.

\begin{corollary}[Generic GR curvature is kinematically available]
For every local GR tetrad, including Schwarzschild, Kerr, FRW, and generic
numerical-relativity tetrads, choose any smooth non-null local $X$.  The
split-jet construction represents that tetrad while the physical curvature is
exactly its Levi--Civita curvature.  No concurrent-vector restriction is
imposed on $\Omega_{\rm phys}$ because the concurrent derivative is
$\widehat D$, not the physical Levi--Civita derivative.
\end{corollary}

\section{Boundary of the result}
The construction is a local right inverse, not a dynamical map
$\Theta\mapsto E$.  Because $K^J$ and $w$ are defined using the prescribed
$E$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:rightinverse} does not select one tetrad among all possible
tetrads.  A complete UBT derivation still needs an action or constraint
principle that:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item selects the split jet structure without turning $K^J$ or $w$ into a
 propagating physical field;
 \item supplies Einstein curvature dynamics with the correct sign and Newton
 coefficient;
 \item gives a well-posed degree-of-freedom count and on-shell solutions.
\end{enumerate}
The construction also requires patching through $X^2=0$ or using complementary
non-null representatives.
