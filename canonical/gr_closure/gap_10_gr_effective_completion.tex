\begin{abstract}
This note combines the exact split-jet auxiliary theorem with the corrected
induced-gravity coefficient.  It states the strongest complete GR result that
is presently justified: a local, generic-curvature, torsion-free Einstein
infrared branch exists as a mathematically closed effective theory under an
explicit spectral/Hessian assumption.  The note also records why this is not
yet an unconditional single-$\Theta$ derivation.
\end{abstract}

Let $e$ be the physical tetrad, $\mathring\omega(e)$ its Levi--Civita
connection, and let $S_{\rm aux}$ be the split-jet multiplier action of
\texttt{gap\_10t\_split\_jet\_auxiliary\_completion.tex}.  Under the specified
one-loop assumptions of
\texttt{gap\_10d\_induced\_gravity\_endgame.tex}, the infrared effective action
through two derivatives is
\begin{equation}
 \Gamma_{\rm IR}
 =-\frac1{16\pi G_{\rm ind}}
 \int d^4x\sqrt g\,(R-2\Lambda_{\rm ren})
 +S_{\rm aux}+S_{\rm matter}
 +O(R^2/\Lambda^2,\nabla^4/\Lambda^2).
 \label{eq:IR}
\end{equation}
Here $G_{\rm ind}$ is the exact regulated expression
\begin{equation}
 \frac1{G_{\rm ind}}
 =\frac{N_B(1-6\xi)}{12\pi}
 \int_{\Lambda^{-2}}^\infty ds\,s^{-2}e^{-m^2s}
 \vartheta_3(0,e^{-s/R_\psi^2}).
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional effective GR closure]
On a non-null split-jet patch, variation of Eq.~\eqref{eq:IR} gives:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item the exact split-jet representation
 $\widehat D_\mu X=\sqrt{\mathcal N_0}e_\mu$;
 \item vanishing multiplier $\lambda=0$, so the jet variables are
 nonpropagating and do not source the metric or physical spin connection;
 \item the torsion-free Levi--Civita physical connection;
 \item the Einstein equation
 \begin{equation}
 G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda_{\rm ren}g_{\mu\nu}
 =8\pi G_{\rm ind}T_{\mu\nu}
 +O(\nabla^4/\Lambda^2).
 \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}
The allowed local curvature is as general as in GR because the split-jet map is
a right inverse for every tetrad.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[GR recovery versus prediction of $G$]
Recovery of general relativity as an infrared effective theory requires a
finite positive renormalized coefficient $G_{\rm R}$ in the Einstein equation;
it does not require the low-energy theory to predict the numerical value of
that dimensionful coefficient.  Equation~\eqref{eq:IR} therefore closes the
conditional GR-recovery problem.  Predicting $G_{\rm R}$ from the fundamental
$\Theta$ measure is a strictly stronger UV-completion problem and remains
open.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The auxiliary Euler--Lagrange theorem gives the constraint and $\lambda=0$.
All auxiliary contributions to the tetrad and connection equations therefore
vanish on shell.  The remaining two-derivative variation is the standard
variation of the Einstein--Hilbert functional.  Universal local
representability follows from the split-jet right inverse.
\end{proof}
