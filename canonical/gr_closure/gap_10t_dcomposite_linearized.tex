\begin{abstract}
We audit the last unanalyzed variational scheme of the minimal branch:
the self-consistent $D$-composite system in which the connection sits
inside the defining jet.  Exact results are obtained at the linearized,
frozen-coefficient level around the affine point.  (i) Imposing
$\Theta\in W_L$ defines a consistent Lorentz-real subsector; necessity
for self-consistent solutions is \emph{not} proved, and a constant
Hermitian shift at a constant tetrad is an explicit counterexample.
(ii) The frozen symbol $A(s,\lambda)$ satisfies
$A^3=(\lambda\!\cdot\!s)A^2$ and
$\operatorname{tr}A^k=6(\lambda\!\cdot\!s)^k$ for $k=1,2,3$.
Its characteristic polynomial is $t^{10}(t-q)^6$ with
$q=\lambda\!\cdot\!s$ and $\det(I-A)=(1-q)^6$.  Generically the
generalized zero-eigenspace contains three size-two and four size-one
Jordan blocks.  (iii) Off $q=1$ the unique driven real-exponential
solution is the exact gradient and is pullback-flat.  For every real
Fourier covector $k$, $q=i\lambda\!\cdot\!k$ and the determinant never
vanishes, so this frozen torsion-free $W_L$ linearization has no nonzero
homogeneous curved real-frequency mode.  (iv) At $q=1$ there is an
exactly six-dimensional real-exponential eigenspace.  Its curl map is
injective, and at several exact generic points its linearized Riemann
map has rank six.  Exceptional loci, variable-coefficient or nonlinear
assembly, the quadratic action, gauge and ghost content, on-shell
torsion, and the chain-rule spin current remain open.  All algebraic
statements are verified in
\texttt{tools/verify\_dcomposite\_linearized.py}.
\end{abstract}

\section{The self-consistent system}
The scheme couples the covariant jet back to the connection:
\begin{equation}
  E_\mu=\mathcal N_0^{-1/2}\bigl(\partial_\mu\Theta
  +\Omega_\mu[E] \,\Theta+\Theta\,\Omega_\mu[E]^{\dd}\bigr),
  \qquad
  g_{\mu\nu}=\tfrac12\bigl(E_\mu^\sharp E_\nu+E_\nu^\sharp E_\mu\bigr),
  \label{eq:selfcons}
\end{equation}
with $\Omega_\mu[E]$ the spin representation of the Levi--Civita
connection of the tetrad defined by the slice coordinates of $E_\mu$
(torsion-free branch first; anholonomy, not physical on-shell torsion,
is the requirement for curvature).

\begin{lemma}[$W_L$ subsector]\label{lem:sector}
Demanding $E_\mu\in W_L$ for \emph{every} independently chosen Lorentz
connection forces the Hermitian part of $\Theta$ to vanish; hence
$\Theta\in W_L$ defines a consistent Lorentz-real subsector (exact
solve; check L0).  This does \emph{not} prove necessity for
self-consistent solutions, where only $\Omega=\Omega[E]$ occurs:
$\Theta=\Theta_{\rm aff}+H$ with constant Hermitian $H$ has
$\Omega[E]=0$ and $D_\mu\Theta\in W_L$ although $\Theta\notin W_L$
(check L0b).  All results below are statements about the $W_L$
subsector.
\end{lemma}

\section{Linearization and the frozen symbol}
The affine field $\Theta_{\rm aff}=\Theta_0+\sqrt{\mathcal N_0}\,\bar
E_\nu x^\nu$ with $\Theta_0\in W_L$ is an exact solution of
\eqref{eq:selfcons} with $\Omega=0$.  Linearize
$\Theta=\Theta_{\rm aff}+\varepsilon\theta$,
$E=\bar E+\varepsilon F$, freeze coefficients at $x_0$, and pass to the
real-exponential (Laplace-type) symbol $\partial_\nu\to s_\nu$ with
real $s$.  The system becomes
\begin{equation}
  (I-A(s,\lambda))\,F=\partial\theta',\qquad
  \lambda^a=\text{slice coords of }\Theta_{\rm aff}(x_0)/\sqrt{\mathcal N_0}
  =x_0^a+\theta_0^a,
\end{equation}
where $A$ implements the linearized Levi--Civita connection of the
perturbed tetrad acting on the background through the bimodule.
$\mathcal N_0$ cancels; $A$ is bilinear in $(s,\lambda)$.  Under the
diagonal Lorentz group at the identity-tetrad background the mixed
contraction $q=\lambda^a s_a$ is invariant.

\begin{theorem}[Symbol identity, spectrum, and Jordan structure]
\label{thm:symbol}
$A^3=qA^2$ holds as a polynomial identity in all eight variables
(check D2), and $\operatorname{tr}A^k=6q^k$ for $k=1,2,3$
(check D2b).  Hence the characteristic polynomial is
$t^{10}(t-q)^6$ and
\begin{equation}
  \det(I-A)=(1-q)^6.
\end{equation}
At generic points $\operatorname{rank}A=9$ and
$\operatorname{rank}A^2=6$ (check D3), so the minimal polynomial is
$t^2(t-q)$ and the generalized zero-eigenspace contains three size-two
plus four size-one Jordan blocks.  The frozen full symbol is modewise
invertible iff $q\neq1$; local or global uniqueness for the
variable-coefficient PDE does not follow from this alone.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Frozen real-frequency no-go]\label{cor:fourier}
For a real Fourier covector $k$, substitute $s=ik$.  Then
$q=i\lambda\!\cdot\!k$ and
\begin{equation}
  \det(I-A(ik,\lambda))=(1-i\lambda\!\cdot\!k)^6\neq0,
\end{equation}
because $|1-ir|^2=1+r^2>0$ for every real $r$.  The homogeneous frozen
symbol therefore has only the zero solution.  Since $A$ annihilates the
exact-gradient image as a polynomial identity (check D1b), every driven
real-frequency response is holonomic and linearly pullback-flat.  Thus
this frozen torsion-free $W_L$ linearization contains no propagating
anholonomic or curved Fourier mode (check D6).
\end{corollary}

\begin{theorem}[Off-$q=1$ flatness]\label{thm:offres}
The linearized Levi--Civita connection factors through the curl of the
tetrad perturbation, so $A$ annihilates every exact gradient as a
polynomial identity (checks D1--D1b).  Consequently, for $q\neq1$ the
unique $\delta\Theta$-driven solution of
$(I-A)F=\partial\theta'$ is $F=\partial\theta'$ itself: exactly
holonomic, with pullback-flat linearized geometry (check D4).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Six-dimensional real-exponential sector]
\label{thm:res}
At $q=1$ the eigenspace $\ker(I-A)$ is exactly six-dimensional
($=\dim\mathfrak{so}(1,3)$).  The curl map is injective: a curl-free
symbol mode is an exact gradient and is therefore annihilated by $A$,
whereas an eigenmode obeys $AF=F$; hence a curl-free eigenmode must
vanish.  Exact multipoint checks D5--D5b confirm rank six.  All
linearized anholonomy of the frozen $D$-composite scheme is confined to
this real-exponential sector.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Where linearized curvature can live]
Any linearized curvature of the frozen scheme must be assembled from
real-exponential symbol modes on the moving surface
$(x_0+\theta_0)\!\cdot\!s=1$.  At several exact generic points the
restricted linearized Riemann map has rank six (check D5c).  This proves
a generic nonzero curvature image, not a theorem excluding exceptional
loci.  The sector is not a real-frequency propagating sector by
Corollary~\ref{cor:fourier}.  Its assembly into a variable-coefficient or
nonlinear curved solution remains open.
\end{corollary}

\section{Answers to the six audit questions at this level}
(1)~Modewise invertibility of the frozen full real-exponential symbol:
yes iff $q\neq1$; PDE-level uniqueness remains open.  For real Fourier
covectors it is invertible for every $k$.
(2)~Anholonomy: driven solutions are exactly holonomic; anholonomy exists
only in the real-exponential $q=1$ sector.
(3)~$R\neq0$ without inserting curvature by hand: the frozen
real-frequency sector is a no-go; a generic nonzero linearized Riemann
image exists only in the real-exponential sector, whose assembly into
curved variable-coefficient solutions is open.
(4)~Chain-rule spin current: not yet computed.
(5)~On-shell torsion selection: not yet computed.
(6)~Degeneracy: outside the exponential $q=1$ sector the scheme
degenerates to the flat driven response.  The six-dimensional remainder
is the only place where the quadratic action can yield nontrivial
linearized geometric dynamics in this subsector.
