\section{Affine and biquaternionic frame connections}
\label{sec:canonical:connection}

The UBT tetrad is $E_\nu=\mathcal N_0^{-1/2}D_\nu\Theta$.  Two descriptions of
classical parallel transport occur:
\begin{itemize}
 \item $\Gamma^\rho{}_{\mu\nu}$ acts on coordinate indices;
 \item $\omega_\mu{}^a{}_b$, and its spin lift $\Omega_\mu$, act on the local
       Lorentz/biquaternionic frame.
\end{itemize}
They are related by
\[
 \partial_\mu e_\nu{}^a-\Gamma^\rho{}_{\mu\nu}e_\rho{}^a
 +\omega_\mu{}^a{}_b e_\nu{}^b=0,
\]
not by $\Gamma=\operatorname{Re}\Omega$.

For a nondegenerate tetrad and specified torsion,
\[
 T^a=de^a+\omega^a{}_b\wedge e^b,
\]
the unique metric-compatible Lorentz connection is
\[
 \boxed{\omega=\mathring\omega(e)+K(T)},
\]
where
\[
 \mathring\omega_\mu{}^a{}_b
 =e_b{}^\nu(\mathring\Gamma^\rho{}_{\mu\nu}e_\rho{}^a
 -\partial_\mu e_\nu{}^a)
\]
and
\[
 K_{abc}=\frac12(T_{cab}-T_{abc}-T_{bca}).
\]
Thus the full metric-compatible connection is kinematically fixed by $(e,T)$,
up to local Lorentz gauge.  In the torsion-free GR branch, $T=0$ and
$\omega=\mathring\omega(e)$.

The remaining UBT question is dynamical: derive torsion and the exact
left/right action on $\Theta$ from the canonical action.  A naive one-sided
regular action is excluded for generic invertible curved configurations under
torsion-free compatibility.  The algebra-native general form is two-sided,
\[
 D_\mu\Theta=\partial_\mu\Theta+A_\mu\Theta-\Theta B_\mu,
 \qquad
 [D_\mu,D_\nu]\Theta
 =F^A_{\mu\nu}\Theta-\Theta F^B_{\mu\nu}.
\]
Lorentz-slice and metric compatibility reduce the no-new-field representative
to
\[
 A_\mu=\Omega_\mu,\qquad B_\mu=-\Omega_\mu^\ddagger,
\]
modulo a common central term that cancels.  This closes the apparent
$A_\mu,B_\mu$ field doubling, but it also implies
$\nabla_\mu V^\nu=\delta_\mu{}^\nu$ for a torsion-free tetrad generated by one
$\Theta$.  It is therefore a proved no-go for generic curved GR and for
Schwarzschild with nonzero mass.  A viable two-sided completion must contain a
nontrivial relative action derived as a composite or auxiliary variable, with
no new propagating degree of freedom.  Curved-space existence of that
self-consistent system remains open.
