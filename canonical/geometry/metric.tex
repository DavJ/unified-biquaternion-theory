\subsection{Canonical definition in the projected GR sector}

The working GR-sector metric is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:canonical:metric_from_theta}
g_{\mu\nu}
=
\frac{\langle \partial_\mu\Theta,\partial_\nu\Theta\rangle_\eta}{\mathcal N},
\qquad
\mathcal N
=
\left|\langle \partial_0\Theta,\partial_0\Theta\rangle_\eta\right|>0.
\end{equation}

with
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:canonical:eta_bilinear}
\langle A,B\rangle_\eta
:=
\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}\left(A^\dagger\eta_{\mathcal B}B\right).
\end{equation}

Here $\eta_{\mathcal B}$ is an external Lorentzian bilinear form on the selected
real four-dimensional projection subspace (equivalently a representation-level
metric operator), not a literal element of $\mathbb B\cong\mathrm{Mat}(2,\mathbb C)$.

\subsection{Symmetry and non-degeneracy}

Symmetry:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:canonical:metric_symmetry}
g_{\mu\nu}=g_{\nu\mu}.
\end{equation}

Lorentzian admissibility requires both:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item real linear independence of projected derivatives $\{\partial_\mu\Theta\}_{\mu=0}^3$,
    \item non-vanishing Lorentzian Gram determinant
    \begin{equation}
    \label{eq:canonical:lorentz_gram_nonzero}
    \det\left(\langle\partial_\mu\Theta,\partial_\nu\Theta\rangle_\eta\right)\neq 0.
    \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}

Non-degeneracy of the induced metric then follows directly:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:canonical:metric_determinant}
\det(g_{\mu\nu})\neq 0.
\end{equation}
