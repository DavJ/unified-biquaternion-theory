# Audit of the frozen covariant-tetrad architecture

**Date:** 2026-07-16  
**Scope:** `papers/UBT_GR_Submission.tex`, the GAP-10Ω and GAP-10I notes,
canonical status surfaces, and agent instructions.  
**Purpose:** distinguish standard tetrad/Cartan geometry from UBT-specific
results, verify the assumptions of the new no-go theorem, and prevent another
framework pivot caused by a formulation artefact.

## Executive verdict

The tetrad pivot is mathematically justified and should be frozen. The earlier
single-section rank obstruction was correct inside an induced-metric/normal-
variation framework, but it was not a no-go theorem for the canonical tetrad
architecture. In the tetrad formulation the relevant local field is the
16-component coframe contained in the four covariant derivatives `E_mu`, and
the metric differential has rank 10 with a six-dimensional local-Lorentz
kernel.

The present route improves the architecture but does not yet close the central
dynamical problem. `GAP-10D`, `GAP-10I-CURVED`, and `GAP-10T-DYN` remain open.
No additional fields, fibers, projections, or embeddings should be introduced
merely because one of these gaps proves difficult.

## Standard geometry versus new UBT content

### Standard material requiring ordinary citations

- tetrad postulate and relation between `Gamma`, `omega`, and `Omega`;
- uniqueness of the torsion-free Levi-Civita spin connection;
- decomposition of a metric-compatible connection into Levi-Civita plus
  contorsion;
- preservation of the Lorentz metric by an `so(1,3)` connection.

These facts should be presented as imported mathematical infrastructure, not as
new discoveries of UBT.

### UBT-specific results or architectural choices

- identifying the UBT covariant first jet with a Lorentz tetrad;
- using the central biquaternionic anticommutator as the metric readout;
- the UBT interpretation of the rank theorem;
- the affine single-`Theta` Minkowski representer;
- the conditional one-sided flatness obstruction;
- the left/right curvature-intertwiner condition as the current minimal
  algebra-native curved candidate.

## Methodological resolution

The repository now adopts two mandatory rules:

1. **Architecture before repair.** Before adding structure, verify whether the
   obstruction is an artefact of the formulation used to state it.
2. **v10.x framework freeze.** The covariant-tetrad route cannot be replaced by
   an AI agent. A pivot requires an explicit author decision and a written
   comparative audit.

## Remaining audit queue
