## 2. ΔV as Transition Barrier

In the speculative psychon framework (see `psychons.tex`), the quantity

    ΔV(p) = V(p; B(p)) − V(p₀; B(p₀))

may be interpreted as the relative potential barrier separating sector p from
the reference sector p₀ = 137.

- **ΔV > 0**: transition from p₀ to p requires work; sector p is "less stable"
  relative to the reference.
- **ΔV < 0**: sector p is energetically favoured relative to p₀.
- **ΔV = 0**: sector p = p₀ (reference / ground state by convention).

This is analogous to free-energy differences in statistical mechanics, but no
thermodynamic interpretation is claimed here.
