## 1. Result

This note closes the first construction gap in
`SPHERICAL_TANGENTIAL_NULL_SHELL.md`: one global smooth angular field can realise

\[
\gamma_{AB}=0,
\qquad
\mathcal B_{S^2}\ne0,
\qquad A,B\in\{\theta,\phi\},

The construction is an exact off-shell field ansatz.  It does not close the

Throughout this note the profile average is

\[

multiplying `Theta` by `sqrt(N_0)`.

## 2. Null biquaternionic plane

Use

\[
q=\mathbf e_2+i\mathbf e_3,
\qquad
p=\mathbf1-i\mathbf e_1,
\]

and define

\[
Q:=q^\sharp p=-2\mathbf e_2-2i\mathbf e_3.
\]

For the central symmetric bilinear

\[
B(a,b)\mathbf1=\frac12(a^\sharp b+b^\sharp a)
\]

one has

\[
B(q,q)=B(p,p)=B(q,p)=0,
\qquad
p^\sharp q=-Q.
\]

Thus every pair of vectors in `span_C{q,p}` has zero central symmetric
pairing, while its ordered antisymmetric product can remain nonzero.

Attach opposite internal Fourier modes,

\[
N_+(\psi)=e^{i\psi}q,
\qquad
N_-(\psi)=e^{-i\psi}p.
\]

The opposite modes retain the averaged ordered product

\[
\langle N_+^\sharp N_-\rangle_\psi=Q,
\]

while all central pairings still vanish.

## 3. Global angular map: Whitney-type immersion

Write the unit sphere using its global Cartesian coordinate functions

\[
n_0=\cos\theta,
\qquad
n_1=\sin\theta\cos\phi,
\qquad
n_2=\sin\theta\sin\phi,
\qquad
n_0^2+n_1^2+n_2^2=1.
\]

Define two globally smooth complex functions

\[
w_1=(1+i n_0)n_1,
\qquad
w_2=(1+i n_0)n_2.
\]

The map `w=(w_1,w_2):S^2 -> C^2` is an immersion.  It has one internal double
point, because the north and south poles both map to `(0,0)`, but its
differential is nonsingular at both poles.  Injectivity of the internal map is
not required for the local tetrad construction.

The angular field is

\[
\boxed{
\Theta_W(\theta,\phi,\psi)
=w_1(\theta,\phi)N_+(\psi)
+w_2(\theta,\phi)N_-(\psi).
}
\]

It is a single globally defined smooth function of the sphere coordinates;
there is no patchwise definition of `Theta_W`.

Because it is written as one potential, its angular jets are integrable by
construction:

\[
E_A^{W}=\partial_A\Theta_W,
\qquad
\partial_\theta E_\phi^{W}
=\partial_\phi E_\theta^{W}.
\]

## 4. Exact null metric and nonzero area channel

Since both angular derivatives lie in the null plane generated by `q,p`,

\[
\boxed{
\gamma^W_{\theta\theta}
=\gamma^W_{\theta\phi}
=\gamma^W_{\phi\phi}=0.
}
\]

The ordered antisymmetric two-form is

\[
\mathcal B_W
=\frac12\left\langle
E_A^{W\sharp}E_B^W-E_B^{W\sharp}E_A^W
\right\rangle_\psi dx^A\wedge dx^B
=dw_1\wedge dw_2\,Q.
\]

Direct differentiation gives

\[
dw_1\wedge dw_2
=K(n_0)\,\omega_{S^2},
\]

where

\[
\omega_{S^2}=\sin\theta\,d\theta\wedge d\phi,
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
K(c)=(1+ic)\left[c+i(2c^2-1)\right].
}
\]

For real `c in [-1,1]`, neither factor can vanish: the first has real part one,
and the second would require simultaneously `c=0` and `2c^2-1=0`.
Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal B_W=K(n_0)\,\omega_{S^2}\,Q
\quad\hbox{is nonzero at every point of }S^2.
}
\]

In spherical coordinates the component multiplying
`dtheta wedge dphi` contains `sin(theta)` and therefore appears to vanish at
the poles.  That is only the usual coordinate degeneration of `(theta,phi)`;
the invariant two-form relative to `omega_{S^2}` is regular and nonzero there.

The total complex flux vanishes,

\[
\int_{S^2}dw_1\wedge dw_2=0,
\]

as it must for an exact two-form on a closed sphere.  Hence this witness carries
no protected net Chern charge; stability must come from dynamics rather than
from this integral.

## 5. Orthogonal profile sectors for the visible exterior

Choose central scalar profiles

\[
\begin{aligned}
P_0&=1,\\
P_c&=\sqrt2\cos(2\psi),&
P_s&=\sqrt2\sin(2\psi),\\
P_t&=\sqrt2\cos(3\psi),&
P_r&=\sqrt2\sin(3\psi).
\end{aligned}
\]

They obey

\[
\langle P_aP_b\rangle_\psi=\delta_{ab}
\]

within the displayed sets, and are profile-orthogonal to the null modes
`e^(+/- i psi)`.  Define

\[
V(\theta,\phi,\psi)
=n_0P_0+n_1P_c+n_2P_s.
\]

Then

\[
\langle V^2\rangle_\psi=1,
\qquad
\langle dV\,dV\rangle_\psi
=d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2,
\qquad
\langle V\,dV\rangle_\psi=0.
\]

Because `V` is central, it gives no antisymmetric biquaternionic channel.

## 6. Complete shell ansatz

Let `R_1<R_2`, `L=R_2-R_1`, and

\[
u=\frac{r-R_1}{L}.
\]

A convenient smooth switch is

\[
b(u)=
\begin{cases}
0,&u\le0,\\
e^{-1/u},&u>0,
\end{cases}
\qquad
H(u)=\frac{b(u)}{b(u)+b(1-u)}.
\]

Thus `H=0` inside the inner radius, `H=1` outside the outer radius, and all
switch derivatives vanish at both boundaries.

Set

\[
\rho(r)=rH(u),
\qquad
\chi(r)=\chi_0[1-H(u)],
\qquad
F'(r)=1-H(u).
\]

The global shell field is

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\Theta={}&it\,P_t+F(r)P_r+\rho(r)V\\
&+\chi(r)\left[w_1e^{i\psi}q+w_2e^{-i\psi}p\right].
\end{aligned}
}
\]

All terms belong to the one UBT field `Theta`; the profile functions merely
select mutually orthogonal internal Fourier sectors.

In the flat ambient profile frame take `E_mu=partial_mu Theta`.  Exact profile
averaging gives

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
ds_\gamma^2={}&-dt^2+B(r)dr^2
+\rho(r)^2\left(d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2\right),\\
B(r)={}&[1-H(u)]^2+[H(u)+r\,\partial_rH(u)]^2.
\end{aligned}
}
\]

All central off-diagonal components vanish.  The null Whitney term contributes
nothing to the central metric, including the radial metric, because its whole
coefficient space is totally isotropic.

At the inner boundary,

\[
H=0,
\quad
\rho=0,
\quad
B=1,
\]

so

\[
ds_\gamma^2\big|_{R_1}=-dt^2+dr^2
\]

and the two angular directions are central-metrically null.  Nevertheless

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal B_{S^2}\big|_{R_1}
=\chi_0^2K(n_0)\omega_{S^2}Q\ne0
}
\]

at every point of the sphere.

At and outside the outer boundary,

\[
H=1,
\quad
\chi=0,
\quad
\rho=r,
\quad
B=1,
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{
ds_\gamma^2=-dt^2+dr^2+r^2d\Omega^2,
\qquad
\mathcal B_W=0.
}
\]

Thus the ansatz joins the tangential-null inner surface to an exactly flat
central exterior with no residual Whitney bivector channel at `R_2`.

## 7. Smooth extension through the centre

The displayed `chi(r) Theta_W` is intended primarily for the shell and inner
boundary.  If the field is extended all the way to `r=0`, smoothness is
obtained by separating the degree-one and degree-two spherical pieces:

\[
\Theta_W^{\rm int}
=\left[a(r)n_1+i b(r)n_0n_1\right]e^{i\psi}q
+\left[a(r)n_2+i b(r)n_0n_2\right]e^{-i\psi}p,
\]

with

\[
a(r)=O(r),
\qquad
b(r)=O(r^2)
\quad(r\to0),
\]

and with `a=b=chi` in a collar of `R_1`.  Standard smooth bump interpolation
provides such functions with all derivatives matched at the collar.  This
keeps the central angular channel null because every derivative remains in
`span_C{q,p}`.

The interior completion is not unique and its dynamics are not fixed by the
kinematic construction.

## 7.5 Nonzero Hermitian support area

\[

(E_A^\ddagger E_B)\right\rangle_\psi.

\[
\begin{aligned}

\[
