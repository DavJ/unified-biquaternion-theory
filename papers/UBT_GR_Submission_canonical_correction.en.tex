\section{Scope and normative status}
This companion correction is normative for the Schwarzschild-status paragraph
of \path{papers/UBT_GR_Submission.tex}.  It does not change the proved local
tetrad, connection, contortion, or split-jet theorems.  It corrects the status
of the older ansatz in
\path{canonical/geometry/biquaternionic_vacuum_solutions.tex} and of
\path{tools/verify_schwarzschild_theta.py}.

The authoritative status remains
\[
 \boxed{\texttt{GAP-U2Theta: OPEN}.}
\]
Canonical on-shell generation of the complete Schwarzschild tetrad and lapse
has not been derived.

% BILINGUAL-UNIT: correction.psi
\section{The missing imaginary-time dependence}
The historical source writes
\begin{equation}
 \Theta_0(r,\tau)=e^{i\alpha(r)}X(r).
 \label{eq:legacy-ansatz}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:legacy-ansatz} has no displayed dependence on $\psi$.
Therefore
\begin{equation}
 \partial_\psi\Theta_0=0,
 \label{eq:psi-zero}
\end{equation}
not $\partial_\psi\Theta_0=i\alpha(r)\Theta_0$.  Merely declaring
$\tau=t+i\psi$ does not supply the missing dependence.  Consequently the
historical step $g_{tt}=-\alpha(r)^2$ does not follow from that ansatz.

% BILINGUAL-UNIT: correction.phase
\section{The omitted radial-phase derivative}
For a nonconstant radial phase, direct differentiation gives
\begin{equation}
 \partial_i\Theta_0=e^{i\alpha(r)}\left[
 \partial_iX+i\alpha'(r)\frac{x_i}{r}X\right].
 \label{eq:phase-derivative}
\end{equation}
The second term in Eq.~\eqref{eq:phase-derivative} was omitted by the claimed
spatial phase cancellation.  Hence the phase-free spatial calculation cannot
simultaneously be used with a radially varying phase without redoing the
metric and cross-term calculation.

% BILINGUAL-UNIT: correction.slice
\section{Canonical Lorentz-slice obstruction}
The numerical script checks the phase-free real-quaternion field
\begin{equation}
 \Theta_0(r)=f(r)\mathbf1+g(r)\mathbf e_r,
 \qquad
 g(r)=r\left(1+\frac{M}{2r}\right)^2,
 \qquad
 f'(r)=\left(1+\frac{M}{2r}\right)\sqrt{\frac{2M}{r}}.
 \label{eq:legacy-spatial}
\end{equation}
At $M=1$, $r=2M$, and on the positive $x$ axis, the scalar coefficient of
$\partial_x\Theta_0$ equals
\begin{equation}
 f'(2M)=\frac54.
 \label{eq:slice-witness}
\end{equation}
It is real and nonzero.  The canonical Lorentz slice instead requires the
scalar coefficient to have the form $i e^0$ with $e^0\in\mathbb R$.
Therefore the derivatives used by the legacy spatial checker are generically
outside the canonical Lorentz slice $W_L$.

The same exact point also contradicts the historical auxiliary assertion
$|X|=1$, because
\begin{equation}
 g(2M)=\frac{25}{8},
 \qquad |X|^2=f^2+g^2\geq\frac{625}{64}>1.
 \label{eq:norm-witness}
\end{equation}

% BILINGUAL-UNIT: correction.verdict
\section{Corrected verdict}
The identity
\begin{equation}
 f'(r)^2+g'(r)^2=\left(1+\frac{M}{2r}\right)^4
 \label{eq:spatial-identity}
\end{equation}
is valid and the legacy script reproduces the positive spatial quadratic form
$g_{ij}=\Psi^4\delta_{ij}$.  This limited identity is not a canonical
single-$\Theta$ Schwarzschild solution, does not determine the lapse, and is
not an on-shell result.

Two later results remain valid within their stated scope: the
composite-contortion theorem represents a prescribed Schwarzschild tetrad
locally but generally with composite torsion, while the split-jet theorem
keeps the physical connection Levi--Civita but its surjective auxiliary
constraint cannot select the tetrad.  Neither result closes
\texttt{GAP-U2Theta}.
