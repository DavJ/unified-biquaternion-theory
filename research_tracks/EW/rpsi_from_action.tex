\section{Reduced action and minimisation equation}
Starting from the compactified ansatz on $M^4\times S^1_\psi$,
\[
S_{\mathrm{eff}}[R_\psi]=\int_{M^4}\Bigl[\frac{R}{2\kappa}+(2\pi R_\psi)\,\mathrm{Tr}((D_\mu\Theta)^\dagger D^\mu\Theta)+V_{\mathrm{Casimir}}(R_\psi)\Bigr]d^4x.
\]
Stationarity condition:
\[
\frac{\partial S_{\mathrm{eff}}}{\partial R_\psi}=0.
\]

\section{Casimir input [STD] and limitation}
For idealised free sectors on a circle,
\[
V_{\mathrm{Casimir}}(R_\psi)\propto -\frac{N_{\mathrm{eff}}}{R_\psi^2}
\]
(regularised zeta/Casimir form [STD]).
This gives a formal scale equation but not yet a unique canonical $R_\psi^*$ from audited UBT inputs alone, because the effective coefficients and sector weights remain model-dependent in the current files.

\section{Implication for EW1+RG branch}
The relation
\[
M_{\mathrm{GUT}}\sim \frac{1}{R_\psi}
\]
is structurally consistent, but the map to the standard GUT window remains conditional until the $R_\psi$ minimisation coefficients are fully fixed from the canonical action normalisation.
