\begin{abstract}
Three charged lepton mass ratios are expressed using only UBT-derived
constants $\pi$, $\alpha$ [\Lo], $N_c=3$ [\Lz], $N_{\rm eff}=12$ [\Lo]:
\begin{align*}
  \frac{m_\tau}{m_\mu}
  &= \frac{1}{N_c(\pi-3)^2}\!\left(1+\tfrac{\pi}{2}\alpha\right)
  = 16.81695\ldots \quad (\text{exp.\ }16.81703,\;{-}0.0005\%), \\[4pt]
  \frac{m_\mu}{m_e}
  &= \frac{1}{N_{\rm eff}(\pi-3)^4}
  = 207.327\ldots \quad (\text{exp.\ }206.768,\;{+}0.27\%), \\[4pt]
  \frac{m_\tau}{m_e}
  &= \frac{1}{N_c N_{\rm eff}(\pi-3)^6}\!\left(1+\tfrac{\pi}{2}\alpha\right)
  = 3486.6\ldots \quad (\text{exp.\ }3477.2,\;{+}0.27\%).
\end{align*}
No free parameter. Status: [\MC] --- structural evidence pending
derivation from $S[\Theta]$.
\end{abstract}

\begin{remark}[Physical interpretation]
The factor $(\pi-3)^2$ per generation step arises from the eta-mode
occupation: $\pi-3 = 2\cdot12\pi\,\Omega_\eta$ [\Lz].
The colour prefactor $N_c=3$ appears for the $\tau/\mu$ ratio
(the heaviest lepton couples most strongly to the colour background).
The mode-count $N_{\rm eff}=12$ suppresses the $\mu/e$ ratio.
The QED one-loop correction $(1+\tfrac\pi2\alpha)$ brings the
$\tau/\mu$ ratio from $-1.1\%$ to $-0.0005\%$.
\end{remark}
