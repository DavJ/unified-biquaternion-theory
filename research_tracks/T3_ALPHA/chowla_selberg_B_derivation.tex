\section{Step 1: \(Z_{T^2}(1)\) baseline via Chowla--Selberg}
For square torus radius \(R\):
\[
Z_{T^2}(1)=R^{-2}Z_{\mathbb{Z}^2}(1),
\qquad Z_{\mathbb{Z}^2}(1)=\pi\ \text{[STD]}.
\]
Hence
\[
Z_{T^2}(1)=\frac{\pi}{R^2},
\qquad
Z_{T^2}(1)\big|_{R\Lambda=1}=\pi\Lambda^2.
\]
This fixes the determinant normalization scale at self-duality.

\section{Step 2: divisor-sum \(n\ln n\) coefficient}
Using
\[
V_{\mathrm{eff}}(n)=-\sum_{d\mid n} Z'_{T^2}(0;d^2/R^2),
\]
and Chowla--Selberg asymptotics
\(Z'_{T^2}(0;d^2/R^2)\sim -2\ln(d/R)+\text{const}\), one obtains
\[
\tau(n):=\sum_{d\mid n}1,
\qquad
\sum_{d\mid n}\ln d = \frac{1}{2}\,\tau(n)\ln n + O(\tau(n)),
\]
so the one-channel asymptotic coefficient of \(n\ln n\) is fixed to unity [L1 asym.].
Therefore the large observed \(B\)-value cannot come from this step alone.

\section{Step 3: normalization factors from phases and modular data}
The single-channel coefficient must be lifted by UBT normalization factors:
\[
B\sim N_{\mathrm{phases}}\times(\text{single-channel coefficient})\times(\text{modular normalizer}).
\]
With \(N_{\mathrm{phases}}=3\) and
\(\vartheta_3(0|i)\) from self-dual heat kernel,
\[
K_{S^1}(t_*)=\vartheta_3(0|i)\quad\text{[STD]}.
\]
The remaining unresolved part is the exact Mellin/spectral insertion proving simultaneously the exponent \(1/4\) and the factor \(2^{1/8}\) in the same renormalization chain.

\section{Step 4: closure status of target coefficient}
Target:
\[
B_{\mathrm{target}}=12^{3/2}\,2^{1/8}\,\vartheta_3(0|i)^{1/4}.
\]
Current result:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \(\mathbb{Z}^2\) determinant chain and \(\Gamma(1/4)\leftrightarrow\eta(i)\leftrightarrow\vartheta_3(0|i)\) bridge are fixed.
  \item \(n\ln n\) scaling source is fixed by divisor asymptotics.
  \item exact determinant-to-coefficient insertion in UBT normalization remains open.
\end{itemize}
