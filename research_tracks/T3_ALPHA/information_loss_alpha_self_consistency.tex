\begin{abstract}
This research note refines the UBT fine-structure constant route by combining
the Layer1 eta/theta spectrum with the Layer2 observable-sector
coding/projection layer.  The eta-winding coefficient is
\[
  B(\rho)=12^{3/2}\bigl(2\eta(i\rho)\bigr)^{1/4},
\]
where $\rho$ is the effective quasi-periodic theta-modulus ratio.  The
stationary winding index $n$ is defined by
\[
  \frac{2n}{\ln n+1}=B(\rho).
\]
The self-dual value $\rho=1$ gives $n=136.989099634\ldots$.  A minimal
four-channel information-loss ansatz gives $n=137.0368227057\ldots$.

The present note introduces a Layer1/Layer2 correction
\[
  C_Q(n)
  =
  4-\frac{\pi-3}{2}\frac{n-r_{\rm L2}}{n}.
\]
Here
\[
  \frac{\pi-3}{2}
  =
  12\pi
  \sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\frac{m}{e^{2\pi m}-1}
\]
is the self-dual eta-mode occupation subtraction.  The simplest sharp-rank
Layer2 approximation is $r_{\rm L2}=3$, giving
$n=137.035999142931\ldots$.  A first eta-kernel refinement replaces the sharp
rank by
\[
  r_{\rm L2}^{(1)}=3\bigl(1+\Omega_\eta(1)\bigr),
  \qquad
  \Omega_\eta(1)=
  \sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\frac{m}{e^{2\pi m}-1}
  =
  \frac{1}{24}-\frac{1}{8\pi}.
\]
This yields
\[
  n_{\rm UBT}=137.035999177549\ldots .
\]
Relative to CODATA/NIST 2022,
$\alpha^{-1}=137.035999177(21)$, this is approximately $+0.026\sigma$.
The result remains research-track: the remaining theorem is to derive the
Layer2 readout kernel and hence $r_{\rm L2}$ from canonical UBT.
\end{abstract}

\section{Separation of indices}

We distinguish three quantities:
\[
  m \in \mathbb{N}
\]
for eta/theta product modes,
\[
  n=\alpha^{-1}
\]
for the effective stationary winding index, and
\[
  \rho=\frac{R_\tau}{R_\psi}
\]
for the effective quasi-periodic theta-modulus ratio.  The eta/theta product
index $m$ is not identified with $n\approx137$.  Instead, the full eta
spectrum contributes to $B(\rho)$, and the stationary equation determines the
effective physical index $n$.

\section{Layer1 eta spectrum}

For $\tau=i\rho$ the Dedekind eta function is
\[
  \eta(i\rho)
  =
  e^{-\pi\rho/12}
  \prod_{m=1}^{\infty}
  \left(1-e^{-2\pi m\rho}\right).
\]
The logarithm is
\[
  L(\rho)=\log\eta(i\rho)
  =
  -\frac{\pi\rho}{12}
  +
  \sum_{m=1}^{\infty}
  \log\left(1-e^{-2\pi m\rho}\right).
\]
Differentiation gives
\[
  \frac{dL}{d\rho}
  =
  -\frac{\pi}{12}
  +
  2\pi
  \sum_{m=1}^{\infty}
  \frac{m}{e^{2\pi m\rho}-1}.
\]
The denominator is $e^{2\pi m\rho}-1$.

At the self-dual point $\rho=1$ the Eisenstein identity
\[
  E_2(i)=\frac{3}{\pi}
\]
implies
\[
  \frac{d}{d\rho}\log\eta(i\rho)\bigg|_{\rho=1}
  =
  -\frac14.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
  \Omega_\eta(1):=
  \sum_{m=1}^{\infty}
  \frac{m}{e^{2\pi m}-1}
  =
  \frac{1}{24}-\frac{1}{8\pi}.
\]
Therefore
\[
  12\pi\,\Omega_\eta(1)
  =
  \frac{\pi-3}{2}.
\]

\begin{remark}
The factor $(\pi-3)/2$ is not fitted.  It is the self-dual eta-mode occupation
subtraction derived from the Layer1 eta spectrum.
\end{remark}

\section{Eta-winding stationary equation}

The eta-corrected winding coefficient is
\[
  B(\rho)
  =
  12^{3/2}
  \left(2\eta(i\rho)\right)^{1/4}.
\]
The effective winding potential is modeled so that its stationary condition is
\[
  \frac{2n}{\ln n+1}=B(\rho).
\]
At the self-dual point $\rho=1$, this gives
\[
  n_0=136.989099634108\ldots .
\]

\section{Information loss and four-channel baseline}

The effective quasi-periodic modulus is written as
\[
  \rho=\exp(-\Delta I_Q),
\]
where $\Delta I_Q$ is the average information loss per quasi-periodic theta
cycle.  The coarse one-loop form is
\[
  \Delta I_Q(n)=\frac{C_Q(n)}{2\pi n}.
\]
The minimal Dirac-biquaternionic channel count suggests $C_Q=4$, giving
\[
  n=137.036822705722871\ldots .
\]
This value is close but too high.

\section{Layer2 projection model}

Layer2 is the UBT observable-sector coding/projection layer.  It selects
physical representation subspaces from the larger theta-biquaternionic field.
In the QCD/SU(3) sector the Layer2 construction contains a protected
three-dimensional color-code subspace.  Abstractly,
\[
  \mathcal H_{\rm color}
  =
  \mathrm{span}\{|100\rangle,\ |010\rangle,\ |001\rangle\},
\]
with projector
\[
  \Pi_{\rm color},
  \qquad
  \mathrm{rank}\,\Pi_{\rm color}=3.
\]
In a finite effective winding sector of dimension $n$, the sharp-projector
approximation says that eta-spectral information loss acts on
\[
  I_n-\Pi_{\rm color}.
\]
The normalized complement rank is
\[
  \frac{\mathrm{Tr}(I_n-\Pi_{\rm color})}{\mathrm{Tr}I_n}
  =
  \frac{n-3}{n}.
\]

The corresponding coefficient is
\[
  C_Q^{(0)}(n)
  =
  4-\frac{\pi-3}{2}\frac{n-3}{n}.
\]
It gives
\[
  n=137.035999142931005\ldots ,
\]
or about $-1.62\sigma$ relative to CODATA/NIST 2022.

\section{First Layer2 eta-kernel refinement}

The sharp-rank model is a flat-spectrum approximation.  A more realistic
Layer2 description should use a readout or decoding kernel rather than a pure
integer rank.  Thus we promote the protected rank to an effective rank,
\[
  3\quad\longrightarrow\quad r_{\rm L2}.
\]
The general form is
\[
  C_Q(n)
  =
  4-\frac{\pi-3}{2}\frac{n-r_{\rm L2}}{n}.
\]

The first non-fitted eta-kernel refinement reuses the same self-dual eta
occupation number already present in the Layer1 subtraction:
\[
  \Omega_\eta(1)
  =
  \sum_{m=1}^{\infty}
  \frac{m}{e^{2\pi m}-1}.
\]
We model the Layer2 color readout as three protected color states, each
receiving a small eta-kernel participation contribution $\Omega_\eta(1)$:
\[
  r_{\rm L2}^{(1)}
  =
  3\left(1+\Omega_\eta(1)\right).
\]
Therefore
\[
  r_{\rm L2}^{(1)}
  =
  3\left(1+\frac{1}{24}-\frac{1}{8\pi}\right).
\]

\begin{conjecture}[First Layer2 eta-kernel rank]
The first effective Layer2 protected rank is
\[
  r_{\rm L2}^{(1)}
  =
  3\left(1+\Omega_\eta(1)\right),
\]
where $\Omega_\eta(1)$ is the self-dual eta-mode occupation number.  This
represents the first spectral readout correction to the sharp Layer2
rank-three color-code projector.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}
This is still not a canonical theorem.  It is stronger than an arbitrary
decimal fit because it introduces no new independent constant; it reuses the
same eta-mode occupation number already appearing in the Layer1 spectral
subtraction.  The remaining proof gap is to derive this readout factor from
the Layer2 decoding kernel.
\end{remark}

\section{Interpretation}

The result can be summarized as a Layer1/Layer2 fixed point:
\[
  \text{Layer1 eta/theta spectrum}
  \quad+\quad
  \text{Layer2 observable-sector readout}
  \quad\Rightarrow\quad
  n=\alpha^{-1}.
\]
The modular eta spectrum supplies
\[
  \varepsilon_\eta=\frac{\pi-3}{2},
\]
while the Layer2 readout supplies an effective protected rank
\[
  r_{\rm L2}=3(1+\Omega_\eta(1)).
\]
Thus the fine-structure constant is not identified with a single theta mode.
It appears as a stationary effective winding index generated by the full eta
spectrum after Layer2 observable-sector readout.

\section{Remaining proof gap}

The remaining open step is structural:
\[
  \boxed{
  G137\text{-L2K}:
  \quad
  \text{derive }
  r_{\rm L2}=3(1+\Omega_\eta(1))
  \text{ from the canonical Layer2 readout kernel.}
  }
\]
A canonical proof requires an explicit Layer2 map
\[
  \mathcal E_{\rm L2}:
  \mathbb B\otimes\mathcal H_\Theta
  \longrightarrow
  \mathcal H_{\rm obs}
\]
and a spectral readout formula such as
\[
  r_{\rm L2}
  =
  \frac{
    \sum_m w_m\,\mathrm{Tr}
    \left(\Pi_{\rm color}K_m^\dagger K_m\right)
  }{
    \sum_m w_m
  },
  \qquad
  w_m=\frac{m}{e^{2\pi m}-1},
\]
whose first self-dual approximation yields $3(1+\Omega_\eta(1))$.

Until this is done, the result is a very strong research-track prediction, not
a canonical derivation of $\alpha$.

\section{Conclusion}

This note upgrades the alpha information-loss route from a sharp-rank Layer2
projection to a first eta-kernel Layer2 readout refinement.  The refined
equation predicts
\[
  \alpha^{-1}_{\rm UBT}=137.035999177549\ldots ,
\]
approximately $+0.026\sigma$ above the CODATA/NIST 2022 value.  No fitted
decimal is introduced; the correction reuses the same self-dual eta occupation
number $\Omega_\eta(1)$ already present in the Layer1 spectral subtraction.
Nevertheless, the Layer2 readout kernel must be derived explicitly before the
result can be promoted from research-track status to a canonical alpha
derivation.
