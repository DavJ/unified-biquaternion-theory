\begin{abstract}
This research note formulates a quantified, non-canonical correction mechanism
for the UBT fine-structure constant route.  The starting point is the
eta-winding coefficient
\[
  B(\rho)=12^{3/2}\bigl(2\eta(i\rho)\bigr)^{1/4},
\]
where $\rho$ is interpreted as the effective scale ratio of a quasi-periodic
complex-time/theta monodromy cycle relative to the compact internal phase
cycle.  The uncorrected self-dual sector has $\rho=1$ and yields a stationary
mode near $n=136.9891$.  We introduce an information-loss correction
\[
  \rho = e^{-\Delta I_Q},
\]
where $\Delta I_Q$ is the average information loss per quasi-periodic theta
cycle.  With the one-loop biquaternionic projection ansatz
\[
  \Delta I_Q(n)=\frac{C_Q}{2\pi n},
\]
and the minimal four-channel estimate $C_Q=4$, the resulting closed
self-consistency equation predicts
\[
  n=\alpha^{-1}_{\rm UBT,1loop}=137.0368227057.
\]
This is close to the Thomson-limit value $\alpha^{-1}\approx137.035999084$,
but is not identical.  The exact Thomson-limit value would require
$C_Q\approx3.9307485957$, a $1.73\%$ correction to the minimal four-channel
estimate.  Therefore this note upgrades the alpha route from a numerical
coincidence to a falsifiable research-track mechanism, but it does not close
the alpha derivation gap.
\end{abstract}

\section{Motivation}

The current conservative UBT alpha status is that the fine-structure constant
is not derived.  Several routes select an integer or near-integer structure
close to $137$, but the observed Thomson-limit value is
\[
  \alpha^{-1}_{\rm exp}\simeq 137.035999084.
\]
The purpose of this note is not to declare a proof, but to formulate a
quantified correction layer that naturally lives in the exponent of the theta
function and does not insert the observed value of $\alpha$ as input.

The key idea is that the integer or near-integer winding sector should be
understood as an uncorrected topological core.  The observed low-energy value
may require an additional correction associated with quasi-periodic theta
monodromy, projection from the full biquaternionic state to the observable
sector, and one-loop information loss.

\section{Theta modulus and eta-winding coefficient}

Let
\[
  \rho = \frac{R_\tau}{R_\psi}
\]
denote the ratio between the effective quasi-periodic theta-monodromy scale
and the compact internal phase scale.  The compact phase coordinate $\psi$ is
periodic, while the complex-time/theta direction is quasi-periodic rather than
an ordinary compactification of physical time.  The physical time coordinate
is not assumed to satisfy $t\sim t+T$.

The Dedekind eta function is defined by
\[
  \eta(\tau)=q^{1/24}\prod_{m=1}^{\infty}(1-q^m),
  \qquad q=e^{2\pi i\tau}.
\]
For purely imaginary modulus $\tau=i\rho$,
\[
  q=e^{-2\pi\rho}.
\]
The eta-winding coefficient considered here is
\begin{equation}
  B(\rho)=12^{3/2}\bigl(2\eta(i\rho)\bigr)^{1/4}.
  \label{eq:B-rho}
\end{equation}
At the self-dual uncorrected point $\rho=1$,
\[
  B(1)=46.2808723831807\ldots
\]
The stationary winding equation is
\begin{equation}
  \frac{2n}{\ln n+1}=B(\rho).
  \label{eq:stationary-winding}
\end{equation}
For $\rho=1$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:stationary-winding} gives
\[
  n=136.9890996341\ldots
\]
Thus the uncorrected eta route lands close to, but not exactly at, the
integer/topological value $137$.

\section{Information loss as a quasi-periodic correction}

The theta function contains its modular parameter directly in the exponent.
For a schematic theta expansion,
\[
  \Theta(z,\tau)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}
  \exp\bigl(\pi i n^2\tau+2\pi i n z\bigr),
\]
and for $\tau=i\rho$ the modal weight becomes
\[
  \exp(-\pi n^2\rho).
\]
Therefore a small change in $\rho$ is not a cosmetic geometric deformation; it
changes the information weight of the winding modes in the theta exponent.

\begin{definition}[Cycle information loss]
Let $A_k\in\mathbb B$ be a biquaternionic transport, holonomy, or interaction
operator acting on a state $\Theta_k$, and let $\Pi$ be the projection from the
full biquaternionic state sector to the observable sector.  Define the average
information loss per quasi-periodic theta cycle by
\begin{equation}
  \Delta I_Q =
  \left\langle
  -\log
  \frac{\|\Pi(A_k\Theta_k)\|^2}{\|A_k\Theta_k\|^2}
  \right\rangle_{\rm cycle}.
  \label{eq:delta-IQ-projection}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

The effective quasi-periodic modulus is modeled as
\begin{equation}
  \rho = e^{-\Delta I_Q}.
  \label{eq:rho-info-loss}
\end{equation}
For small information loss,
\[
  \rho\simeq 1-\Delta I_Q,
\]
so that the geometric-looking shift $\rho=1-\delta$ is equivalently an
information-loss shift with $\delta\simeq\Delta I_Q$.

\begin{remark}
Equation~\eqref{eq:delta-IQ-projection} is the microscopic interpretation of
the correction.  It says that the shift of the quasi-periodic theta modulus is
caused by information transferred from the observable projection into hidden
bicomplex/quaternionic components over one theta cycle.
\end{remark}

\section{One-loop biquaternionic projection ansatz}

The natural dimensionless one-loop factor is $1/(2\pi n)$ when
$n=\alpha^{-1}$.  We therefore write
\begin{equation}
  \Delta I_Q(n)=\frac{C_Q}{2\pi n}.
  \label{eq:IQ-one-loop}
\end{equation}
The minimal Dirac-biquaternionic channel count suggests
\begin{equation}
  C_Q=4.
  \label{eq:CQ-four}
\end{equation}
The factor $4$ is not fitted to the observed value of $\alpha$; it is the
minimal four-channel estimate associated with the four effective spinorial or
bicomplex projection channels of the biquaternionic field.

Combining Eqs.~\eqref{eq:B-rho}, \eqref{eq:stationary-winding},
\eqref{eq:rho-info-loss}, and \eqref{eq:IQ-one-loop} gives the closed equation
\begin{equation}
  \frac{2n}{\ln n+1}
  =
  12^{3/2}
  \left[
    2\eta\left(
      i\exp\left[-\frac{C_Q}{2\pi n}\right]
    \right)
  \right]^{1/4}.
  \label{eq:closed-alpha-self-consistency}
\end{equation}
This is the main equation of the note.

\begin{proposition}[Minimal one-loop prediction]
For $C_Q=4$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:closed-alpha-self-consistency} has the solution
\[
  n = 137.0368227057\ldots
\]
near the physical fine-structure constant.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Numerical proof]
Using the product definition of $\eta(i\rho)$ and solving
Eq.~\eqref{eq:closed-alpha-self-consistency} near $n=137$ gives
\[
  n=137.0368227057228716846\ldots
\]
The corresponding information loss and modulus are
\[
  \Delta I_Q=\frac{4}{2\pi n}=0.00464561101022\ldots,
\]
\[
  \rho=e^{-\Delta I_Q}=0.99536516314996\ldots.
\]
\end{proof}

\section{Comparison to the Thomson-limit value}

The CODATA-level Thomson-limit value is approximately
\[
  \alpha^{-1}_{\rm exp}\simeq 137.035999084.
\]
The minimal one-loop estimate differs by
\[
  \Delta n
  =137.0368227057-137.035999084
  \simeq 8.236\times10^{-4},
\]
with relative size
\[
  \frac{\Delta n}{137.036}\simeq 6.0\times10^{-6}.
\]

If the observed value is used only as a post-check, the required effective
channel coefficient is
\begin{equation}
  C_Q^{\rm target}=3.9307485957\ldots
  \label{eq:CQ-target}
\end{equation}
Thus the exact Thomson-limit value would require
\[
  C_Q = 4 - 0.0692514043\ldots,
\]
a $1.73\%$ correction to the minimal four-channel estimate.

\begin{statusbox}[title=Interpretation of the residual]
The $1.73\%$ residual correction in $C_Q$ is not explained in this note.  It
may represent a modular normalization correction, a projection-norm correction,
a non-commutative ordering correction, a two-loop term, or simply evidence that
$C_Q=4$ is only an approximate channel-counting ansatz.
\end{statusbox}

\section{Status and open gap}

This note provides a closed and non-circular research-track equation for
$\alpha$ once the minimal value $C_Q=4$ is assumed.  It upgrades the eta-winding
route from a loose numerical observation to a concrete one-loop self-consistency
mechanism.  However, the alpha derivation gap remains open because the exact
projection coefficient has not yet been derived from the UBT action.

\begin{equation}
  \boxed{
  \text{Open gap G137-IQ: derive } C_Q \text{ from } S[\Theta]
  \text{ and the observable projection } \Pi.
  }
\end{equation}

The strongest conservative statement is therefore
\[
  \boxed{
  \alpha^{-1}_{\rm UBT,1loop}=137.0368227057
  \quad\text{for the minimal } C_Q=4 \text{ information-loss ansatz.}
  }
\]
This is a self-consistent prediction at research-track level, not a canonical
first-principles derivation of the fine-structure constant.
