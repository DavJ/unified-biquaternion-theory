\begin{abstract}
The diagonal determinant attack on G137-B showed that a product determinant
$n!$ gives $\log\Gamma(n+1)$ and fails.  The alpha route requires instead a
leading entropy $n\log n$.  This note derives that leading entropy from the
Layer2 readout structure of an $n$-fold winding cover.  The $n$ coherent turns
of the cover form a sheet set $\mathcal S_n$.  A local Layer2 readout field is
a sheet-valued map from each turn into the same coherent cover,
$f:\mathcal S_n\to\mathcal S_n$.  Hence the readout configuration space is
$\mathrm{End}(\mathcal S_n)$ and has cardinality $n^n$, giving
$\log|\mathrm{End}(\mathcal S_n)|=n\log n$.  If one additionally quotients by a
global deck relabelling of the target sheet origin, the entropy changes only by
$-\log n$, a subleading term.  Therefore the leading $n\log n$ part of G137-B is
closed conditional on the Layer2 local-readout principle.  The eta-dressed
coefficient $B(\rho)$ remains a separate coefficient subgap G137-Bb.
\end{abstract}

\section{The $n$-fold winding cover}

Let the compact $\psi$ direction be a circle.  An $n$-winding configuration is
an $n$-fold coherent cover of the fundamental $\psi$ cycle.  Fix a base point
and an orientation.  The cover has a distinguished finite sheet set
\[
  \mathcal S_n=\{1,2,\ldots,n\}.
\]
The deck group is cyclic, $C_n$, but the local Layer2 readout is performed after
choosing a base sheet.  A later quotient by a global deck relabelling is a
gauge-volume correction and is subleading in the entropy.

\section{Diagonal determinant versus readout field}

A diagonal determinant assumes that sheet $a$ interacts only with the same
level $a$.  This produces
\[
  \prod_{k=1}^{n}k=n!,
\]
and therefore $\log\Gamma(n+1)$.  This was shown to give the wrong stationary
point.

Layer2 readout is different.  It is not a diagonal product over eigenlevels; it
is a local observable assignment over the coherent cover.  Each turn of the
cover must be assigned a readout sheet in the same cover.

\begin{definition}[Layer2 sheet-valued readout field]
For an $n$-fold winding cover, a Layer2 sheet-valued readout configuration is a
map
\[
  f:\mathcal S_n\longrightarrow\mathcal S_n.
\]
The readout configuration space is
\[
  \mathcal R_n:=\mathrm{Map}(\mathcal S_n,\mathcal S_n)
  =\mathrm{End}(\mathcal S_n).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Endomap count]
For $|\mathcal S_n|=n$,
\[
  |\mathrm{End}(\mathcal S_n)|=n^n.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For each of the $n$ elements of $\mathcal S_n$, the value of $f$ can be chosen
independently among $n$ target sheets.  Therefore the total number of maps is
\[
  \underbrace{n\cdot n\cdots n}_{n\text{ times}}=n^n.
\]
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Leading Layer2 winding entropy]
The Layer2 sheet-valued readout field on an $n$-fold coherent winding cover has
leading entropy
\[
  S_{\rm L2}(n)=\log|\mathcal R_n|=n\log n.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By the endomap count,
\[
  |\mathcal R_n|=n^n.
\]
Taking the logarithm gives
\[
  S_{\rm L2}(n)=\log(n^n)=n\log n.
\]
\end{proof}

\section{Gauge-volume correction from deck relabelling}

If the global origin of the target sheet set is considered unphysical, one may
quotient by the free target action of the deck group $C_n$:
\[
  f\sim T^a\circ f,
  \qquad a\in C_n.
\]
For nonzero $a$, $T^a\circ f=f$ has no solution because $T^a$ has no fixed sheet.
Thus the action on maps is free and
\[
  |\mathcal R_n/C_n|=\frac{n^n}{n}=n^{n-1}.
\]
The entropy is then
\[
  \log|\mathcal R_n/C_n|=(n-1)\log n=n\log n-\log n.
\]
The correction $-\log n$ is subleading relative to $n\log n$ and changes only
subleading terms in the effective potential.

\begin{remark}
This resolves an important overcounting objection.  The all-to-all readout does
not rely on treating a pure gauge origin as physical.  Removing the global deck
volume changes the entropy by $O(\log n)$, not by $O(n\log n)$.
\end{remark}

\section{Effective alpha potential}

The self-dual winding stiffness gives
\[
  V_{\rm wind}(n)=n^2
\]
in dimensionless units.  Dressing the Layer2 entropy by the oscillator/readout
index $B(\rho)$ gives
\[
  \log Z_n(\rho)=B(\rho)n\log n+O(\log n)+O(n)_{\rm lin}.
\]
The effective potential is therefore
\[
  V_{\rm eff}(n,\rho)=n^2-B(\rho)n\log n+\text{subleading/linear terms}.
\]
Keeping the leading entropy gives the alpha stationarity equation
\[
  2n-B(\rho)(\log n+1)=0.
\]

\section{What remains: G137-Bb}

This note closes only the entropy-shape subgap:
\[
  \boxed{G137\text{-Ba}:\quad n\log n\text{ from Layer2 endomap readout}.}
\]
The coefficient subgap remains:
\[
  \boxed{G137\text{-Bb}:\quad
  B(\rho)=12^{3/2}(2\eta(i\rho))^{1/4}
  \text{ from the canonical }\Theta\text{ measure}.}
\]
Existing UBT ingredients strongly suggest this factorisation:
\[
  12=N_{\rm eff},
  \qquad
  (2\eta(i\rho))^{1/4}=\text{compact oscillator weight},
\]
but a full canonical measure derivation is still required before alpha can be
promoted from strong MC+/L1-conditional evidence to a theorem.

\section{Conclusion}

The failed diagonal determinant $n!$ and the successful endomap count $n^n$ show
that the alpha route requires Layer2 readout entropy, not a simple spectral
product.  The leading $n\log n$ term is now derived from the natural map space
$\mathrm{End}(\mathcal S_n)$ of the $n$-fold winding cover, with only subleading
changes under deck-group quotienting.  The remaining alpha gap is therefore no
longer the entropy shape but the exact eta/heat-kernel coefficient $B(\rho)$.
