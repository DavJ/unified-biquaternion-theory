\section{Problem statement}

Known datum from previous scans:
\begin{equation}
a_{137}(f)=-11,\qquad f\in S_2(\Gamma_0(76)).
\end{equation}
Question: for the \emph{same} form $f$, what are $a_{131}(f)$ and $a_{139}(f)$?

\section{Analytic constraints}

For good primes $p\nmid 76$, Deligne/Weil bound gives
\begin{equation}
|a_p(f)|\le 2\sqrt{p}.
\end{equation}
Numerically:
\begin{align}
|a_{131}| &\le 22.9,\\
|a_{137}| &\le 23.4,\\
|a_{139}| &\le 23.6.
\end{align}
This does not determine exact values, only admissible range [L0].

\section{Data-access attempt and failure point}

Planned source was LMFDB endpoint
\texttt{https://www.lmfdb.org/ModularForm/GL2/Q/holomorphic/76/2/}.
In this environment DNS resolution for \texttt{www.lmfdb.org} failed, and
Sage/PARI executables are not installed; therefore direct coefficient
extraction for $a_{131},a_{139}$ could not be completed.

Failure location: missing external coefficient source for the fixed newform label.
Classification of the unresolved step: [OPEN].

\begin{proposition}[Prime specificity of $a_{137}$]
Let $f$ be the newform of level $N=76$, weight $k=2$ with $a_{137}(f)=-11$.

Case 1 (if $a_{131}(f)\ne\pm11$ and $a_{139}(f)\ne\pm11$):
$|a_{137}(f)|=g(X_0(137))=11$ is PRIME-SPECIFIC to $p=137$; classification [MC] strong.

Case 2 (if $a_{131}(f)=\pm11$ or $a_{139}(f)=\pm11$):
coincidence is form-level, not $p=137$-specific; classification [NC].

Current run cannot decide between Case 1 and Case 2 due missing $a_{131},a_{139}$ data.
\end{proposition}
