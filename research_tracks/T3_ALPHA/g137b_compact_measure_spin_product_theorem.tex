\begin{abstract}
This note closes the determinant-composition part of G137-Bb at bridge level.
The SU(2) Scherk--Schwarz analysis already decomposes the compact $\Theta$
sector into periodic neutral diagonal blocks and anti-periodic charged
off-diagonal blocks.  The odd-winding/fermionic bridge adds the parity-twisted
sector required by the complete compact readout trace.  The canonical Layer2
readout over the $\mathrm{Im}\,\mathbb H$ triad therefore contains the three
standard torus theta blocks
\[
  \vartheta_3,
  \qquad
  \vartheta_2,
  \qquad
  \vartheta_4,
\]
with two real oscillator denominators $\eta^{-2}$.  Hence
\[
  Z_{\rm compact}^{1\rm real}(\tau)
  =\eta(\tau)^{-2}\vartheta_2(0|\tau)\vartheta_3(0|\tau)\vartheta_4(0|\tau)
  =2\eta(\tau).
\]
Together with the four-channel geometric mean and the base factor
$12^{3/2}$, this gives
\[
  B(\rho)=12^{3/2}(2\eta(i\rho))^{1/4}.
\]
The theorem is L1-conditional: it depends on accepting the existing SU(2) twist,
odd-winding fermionic measure, and complete Layer2 spin-structure readout
bridges as canonical.
\end{abstract}

\section{Closure theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Compact one-real measure]
Assuming the SU(2) twist, odd-winding parity bridge, and complete Layer2
spin-structure trace, the one-real compact readout determinant is
\[
  Z_{\rm compact}^{1\rm real}(\tau)
  =\eta(\tau)^{-2}\vartheta_2(0|\tau)\vartheta_3(0|\tau)\vartheta_4(0|\tau).
\]
By Jacobi's identity, this equals
\[
  Z_{\rm compact}^{1\rm real}(\tau)=2\eta(\tau).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The SU(2) twist decomposes the compact matrix field into periodic neutral and
shifted charged sectors, giving $\vartheta_3$ and $\vartheta_2$.  The
odd-winding fermionic bridge supplies the parity-twisted trace sector, giving
$\vartheta_4$.  The compact determinant has two real oscillator denominators,
giving $\eta^{-2}$.  Therefore
\[
  Z_{\rm compact}^{1\rm real}(\tau)
  =\eta^{-2}\vartheta_2\vartheta_3\vartheta_4.
\]
The standard Jacobi identity $\vartheta_2\vartheta_3\vartheta_4=2\eta^3$ gives
$Z_{\rm compact}^{1\rm real}=2\eta$.
\end{proof}
