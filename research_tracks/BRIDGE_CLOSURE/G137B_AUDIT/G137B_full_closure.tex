\section{A4: SS BC $\Rightarrow$ $\vartheta_3/\vartheta_2$ Split — \Lo}
%──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The SS boundary condition $\Theta(\psi+2\pi R_\psi)=\sigma_3\Theta(\psi)\sigma_3$
[\Lo, \texttt{su2\_twist\_neff12.tex}] acts as:
\[
  \sigma_3\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\sigma_3
  = \begin{pmatrix}a&-b\\-c&d\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Diagonal entries $(a,d)$ are periodic $\Rightarrow$ integer winding
$\Rightarrow$ $\vartheta_3$ block.
Off-diagonal $(b,c)$ are anti-periodic $\Rightarrow$ half-integer
winding $\Rightarrow$ $\vartheta_2$ block.
The $\vartheta_3/\vartheta_2$ split follows directly from the SS BC.
\textbf{A4: \Lo.}
