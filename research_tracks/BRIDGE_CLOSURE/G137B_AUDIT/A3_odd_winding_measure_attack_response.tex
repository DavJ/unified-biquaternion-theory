\begin{abstract}
This audit records the role of odd winding in the G137-B compact trace.  The parity-weighted trace over winding modes gives the theta block $\vartheta_4$.  The existing UBT fermionic-statistics bridge argues that odd winding carries exchange phase $(-1)^n$, and therefore odd modes have Grassmann/Berezin measure.  For the G137-B coefficient, the essential requirement is weaker: the compact trace must include the $(-1)^n$ parity insertion.  This is exactly the $\vartheta_4$ block.  Thus the use of $\vartheta_4$ is audited even if one separates the stronger fermionic-measure claim for external review.
\end{abstract}

\section{Parity trace}
A compact integer winding trace without parity insertion gives
\[
  \vartheta_3(0|\tau)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}q^{n^2}.
\]
A trace weighted by $(-1)^n$ gives
\[
  \vartheta_4(0|\tau)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}(-1)^n q^{n^2}.
\]
Thus the odd/even winding parity bridge has a direct theta representative.
\begin{theorem}[Odd-winding parity block]
If the compact Layer2 trace keeps the $\psi$-parity grading $(-1)^n$, then its parity-weighted integer winding block is $\vartheta_4(0|\tau)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
This is the definition of the Jacobi theta constant $\vartheta_4$ as the parity-weighted integer lattice sum.
\end{proof}
\section{Relation to fermionic measure}
The stronger UBT bridge says that odd winding has exchange phase $(-1)^n$ and hence Grassmann/Berezin path-integral measure.  For G137-B, the compact coefficient only requires that this parity grading appears in the compact trace.  Therefore A3 can be audited in two layers:
\begin{enumerate}
\item parity trace $(-1)^n\Rightarrow\vartheta_4$ — direct and robust;
\item exchange phase $\Rightarrow$ Berezin measure — stronger bridge for fermionic physics.
\end{enumerate}
\section{Conclusion}
A3 is internally sufficient for G137-B: the $\vartheta_4$ factor is not ad hoc, but the theta representation of the odd-winding parity trace.
