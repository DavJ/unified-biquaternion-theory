\begin{abstract}
This file supersedes the March 2026 claim that the kernel of the metric
variation map consists only of $U(2)$ rotations and that this would establish
$\Theta$-only GR closure.  That argument mixed injectivity with surjectivity and
also omitted normal variations of an induced metric.  The correct local rank
statement is given here and the positive fiber-completed result is delegated to
the canonical proof.
\end{abstract}

\section{The logical error}

For a composite action, stationarity yields schematically
\begin{equation}
 \mathscr F_\Theta+J^*\mathscr E_g=0.
\end{equation}
To infer $\mathscr E_g=0$ from $J^*\mathscr E_g=0$, one needs injectivity of
$J^*$, equivalently density/surjectivity of the metric-variation map $J$ onto
the relevant metric variations.  Proving that $J$ has only gauge kernel would
show injectivity of $J$ after quotienting; it does not show surjectivity of $J$
and is not the required theorem.

\section{Single-section rank bound}

At fixed $\psi$, let $\Theta:M^4\to\mathbb B\cong\mathbb R^8$ and
\begin{equation}
 g_{\mu\nu}=H(\partial_\mu\Theta,\partial_\nu\Theta)/\mathcal N_0.
\end{equation}
At a regular point, four tangent vectors span a four-dimensional subspace of
the eight-dimensional carrier, leaving at most four normal directions.  The
normal second-derivative closure map
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal K:\operatorname{Sym}^2(T_xM)\to N_x,
 \qquad X^{\mu\nu}\mapsto X^{\mu\nu}B_{\mu\nu}
\end{equation}
has
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{rank}\mathcal K\le4,
 \qquad \dim\ker\mathcal K\ge6.
\end{equation}
Thus generic pointwise GR closure from one section is impossible in the
canonical eight-real-dimensional core field.

\section{Fiber completion}

UBT already supplies the compact profile $\psi\mapsto\Theta(x,\psi)$.  With the
metric defined by fiber averaging, the carrier becomes the space of such
profiles and has infinitely many independent normal modes.  Ten normal
second-derivative vectors can then be independent.  Existence, openness,
density and the exact first variation are proved in
\begin{center}
\texttt{canonical/gr\_closure/pure\_ubt\_fiber\_closure.tex}.
\end{center}
