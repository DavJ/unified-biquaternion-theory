# GR off-shell closure: revised pure-UBT gap statement

**Date:** 2026-07-14  
**Canonical proof:** `canonical/gr_closure/pure_ubt_fiber_closure.tex`

## What was corrected

The former GAP-10 statement asked whether the kernel of
`J = delta g / delta Theta` contains only gauge directions. That is not the
right criterion for recovering Einstein's equations. To infer the metric
Euler-Lagrange residual vanishes from `J* E_g = 0`, the adjoint `J*` must be
injective on metric residuals; equivalently, the metric variations generated by
Theta must be sufficiently surjective. Kernel control of `J` alone does not
establish this.

## Exact result for one psi section

For a genuinely biquaternion-valued field

```tex
Theta(x,psi_0) in B ~= R^8,
```

a regular four-dimensional tangent space leaves at most four normal directions.
The normal closure map from ten symmetric metric components therefore has rank
at most four. Generic pointwise Theta-only GR closure from a single `psi`
section is impossible.

**Status:** `NO-GO [L1]` for the single-section formulation.

## Exact result for the existing complex-time fiber

UBT already contains the full profile

```tex
psi -> Theta(q_GR(x), x^0 + i psi).
```

Using the normalized `psi`-averaged metric, the carrier is the
infinite-dimensional space of these profiles. The exact first variation gives

```tex
F_Theta + (kappa N_0)^(-1)
  nabla_nu[(G^{mu nu}-kappa T^{mu nu}) partial_mu Theta] = 0.
```

On the direct internal Theta shell, this reduces to

```tex
(G^{mu nu}-kappa T^{mu nu}) B_{mu nu}=0.
```

When the ten normal profile vectors `B_{mu nu}` are linearly independent, the
full Einstein equation follows. Such fiber-free analytic two-jets exist and
are open dense in every sufficiently rich finite-mode holomorphic two-jet truncation.

**Status:** local kinematic/rank closure `CLOSED [L1]` on the fiber-free sector.
