## Honest summary

The track has closed a useful negative/positive pair:

- balanced counter-propagating modes can carry energy and compact pressure with
  zero net compact momentum;
- such scalar pressure alone does not select a rotation direction;
- a common bivector rotor is gauge/frame freedom and leaves the metric fixed.

The remaining potentially new mechanism is therefore sharply localized:

```text
balanced compact modes
+ non-zero covariant internal orientation
+ action-derived coupling
-> symmetric tetrad strain / connection response
-> possible g_0i
```

No step after `non-zero covariant internal orientation` is currently closed.
