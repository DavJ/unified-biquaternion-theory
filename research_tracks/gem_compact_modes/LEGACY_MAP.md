historical, and hypothesis-recovery purposes.  They are not deleted and their
original arguments are not silently rewritten.

## Why they remain valuable

1. They record the historical route through a projected
   biquaternionic/imaginary metric.
2. They contain useful GR examples, causal questions, and candidate failure
   modes.
3. Future canonical architecture may motivate revisiting selected ideas.
4. They show why modern UBT now demands an explicit metric readout and
   action-derived source.

## Architectural distinction

The historical branch often uses a schematic full metric

```tex
Gcal_mu_nu = g_mu_nu + i h_mu_nu + j a_mu_nu + k b_mu_nu
```

and a real projection.  The current covariant-tetrad branch instead uses

```tex
1/2 (E_mu^sharp E_nu + E_nu^sharp E_mu) = g_mu_nu 1.
```

The old branch is therefore classified as **legacy speculative architecture**,
not as disproved mathematics and not as a current canonical source.

## Return conditions

A legacy mechanism may be reactivated when it is supplied with:

- a map into the current tetrad/central-anticommutator architecture, or a clear
  argument for revising that architecture;
- a consistent action and involutions;
- an explicit source and conservation law;
- a proof that its metric response is not a projection or gauge artifact.
