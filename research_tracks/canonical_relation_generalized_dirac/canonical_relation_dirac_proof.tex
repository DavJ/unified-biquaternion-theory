\begin{abstract}
We prove that the canonical UBT relation
$E_\mu=\mathcal N_0^{-1/2}D_\mu\Theta$ and its central biquaternionic metric
identity admit an exact injective block lift to curved Clifford matrices.
The generalized-Dirac structure is therefore an operator completion of the
same covariant tetrad, not a competing spinor-current tetrad.  We also record
the all-nondegenerate-tetrad rank-ten theorem, its six-dimensional Lorentz
kernel, the exact anticommuting fifth/grading channel, and the exact principal-
symbol factorisation showing that the lifted Dirac causal cone equals the
canonical metric null cone.  We also prove a conditional psi-normal-form
first-jet theorem and isolate strict complex-time holomorphy as the remaining
rank gate.  The derivation of the full dynamical operator and its holomorphic
on-shell rank remains open.
\end{abstract}
