# Phase 6 — Bare integer attractor to physical \(\alpha\)

> This phase is secondary and is **not** used to justify \(B_\mathrm{eff}\).

## Framing

Assume a bare integer attractor exists at high scale, then test whether known low-energy corrections can shift to

\[
\alpha^{-1}_{\mathrm{physical}}\approx \alpha^{-1}_{\mathrm{bare}} + 0.036.
\]

## Candidate correction channels

1. **QED running / vacuum polarization**  
   Small logarithmic shifts of inverse coupling at low scales are standard and can naturally generate \(\mathcal O(10^{-2})\)-level corrections.

2. **Finite-size compact-phase effects**  
   Potential subleading \(1/n\)-type corrections in compact geometry; not yet fixed uniquely.

3. **Eta / cusp / modular anomaly residues**  
   Could contribute small additive constants if a modular route is fully derived.

4. **Lepton-threshold effects**  
   Can enter effective running in low-energy normalization.

## Status

This correction-scale problem appears plausible in magnitude, but remains dependent on whichever primary \(B_\mathrm{eff}\) derivation eventually closes. It does not currently close Gap G137-B.

**Phase-6 verdict:** plausible secondary correction program, non-blocking and non-resolving for main gap.
