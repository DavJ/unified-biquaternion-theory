# Phase 4 — Modular-index route (without hand-setting \(N\))

## Known structural clue

For prime \(N\):

\[
\mu(\Gamma_0(N)) = N+1,
\qquad
\frac{\mu(\Gamma_0(N))}{3}=\frac{N+1}{3}.
\]

Numerically, if \(N\) is set to a specific prime associated with the attractor, this lands near the needed \(B\)-window.

## Required non-circular chain

To be valid, the chain must be:

\[
S[\Theta]\ \Rightarrow\ V_{\mathrm{mod}}(N)\ \Rightarrow\ N_*\ \Rightarrow\ B_\mathrm{eff}=\mu(\Gamma_0(N_*))/3 + \delta_{\mathrm{mod}}.
\]

## Current status of each arrow

1. **\(S[\Theta]\to V_{\mathrm{mod}}(N)\):** not explicitly closed canonically.
2. **Dynamic selection of \(N_*\):** not closed without importing attractor target externally.
3. **Residual \(\delta_{\mathrm{mod}}\) (eta/cusp/elliptic/finite-size):** no uniquely fixed value from canonical equations.

## Residual correction question

Even when index/3 gives a near-baseline value, the required residual correction

\[
\delta_B\approx 0.2839
\]

is not yet derived by a unique modular anomaly term fixed by UBT action data.

## Phase-4 verdict

Modular index remains the strongest structural near-hit, but without an internal derivation of \(N\) and \(\delta_B\) it is still **non-closed** as a first-principles derivation.
