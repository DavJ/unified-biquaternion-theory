\section{Twisted boundary condition setup}
Candidate twist on $S^1_\psi$:
\[
\Theta(\psi+2\pi R_\psi)=e^{i\alpha M}\Theta(\psi),
\]
with anti-periodic limit represented by half-integer KK modes.

\section{KK spectrum [STD given twist]}
If anti-periodic boundary conditions hold,
\[
m_n^{\mathrm{twisted}}=\frac{n+1/2}{R_\psi},\qquad n\in\mathbb{Z},
\]
and
\[
K_{S^1}^{\mathrm{twist}}(t)=\sum_n e^{-t(n+1/2)^2/R_\psi^2}=\vartheta_2\!
\left(0\middle|i\frac{t}{\pi R_\psi^2}\right).
\]

\section{Current obstruction}
The missing [L1] step is not the standard KK formula itself, but proving that the involution used for twist is a symmetry of the canonical audited action and yields a fermionic first-order sector (rather than only modified bosonic spectrum) in the same derivation path.
