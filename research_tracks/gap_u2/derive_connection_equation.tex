\section{Covariant Abelian variation}
Use the convention
\[
 D_\mu\Theta=\nabla_\mu\Theta-iqA_\mu\Theta,
 \qquad F_{\mu\nu}=2\nabla_{[\mu}A_{\nu]},
\]
and the real Lagrangian
\[
 \mathcal L=\sqrt{-g}\left[
 \operatorname{ReTr}\bigl((D_\mu\Theta)^\dagger D^\mu\Theta\bigr)
 -\frac14F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}\right].
\]
Then
\[
 \delta(D_\mu\Theta)=-iq\,\delta A_\mu\Theta,
 \qquad
 \delta(D_\mu\Theta)^\dagger=iq\,\delta A_\mu\Theta^\dagger.
\]
The matter variation is therefore
\begin{align}
 \delta_A S_{\Theta}
 &=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\,\delta A_\mu\,
 q\operatorname{ReTr}\!\left[
 i\left(\Theta^\dagger D^\mu\Theta
 -(D^\mu\Theta)^\dagger\Theta\right)\right]\\
 &=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\,J^\mu\delta A_\mu,
\end{align}
where, with the present sign convention,
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{J^\mu
 =q\operatorname{ReTr}\!\left[
 i\left(\Theta^\dagger D^\mu\Theta
 -(D^\mu\Theta)^\dagger\Theta\right)\right]
 =-2q\operatorname{ImTr}(\Theta^\dagger D^\mu\Theta).}
 \label{eq:current}
\end{equation}
Changing the sign convention in $D_\mu$ reverses the overall sign of $J^\mu$.
The previously used expression with $i$ outside
$\operatorname{ReTr}(\cdots)$ is identically zero, because the trace of the
anti-Hermitian difference is purely imaginary before the real projection.

Variation of the Maxwell term and integration by parts give
\[
 \delta_A S_F=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\,
 (\nabla_\nu F^{\nu\mu})\delta A_\mu
\]
up to the boundary term.  Hence the Euler--Lagrange equation is
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\nabla_\nu F^{\nu\mu}+J^\mu=0}
 \label{eq:maxwell}
\end{equation}
for the sign conventions above.  Equivalently one may define
$j^\mu=-J^\mu$ and write $\nabla_\nu F^{\nu\mu}=j^\mu$.

\section{Why this does not derive the Schwarzschild lapse}
For an electrostatic component $A_t(r)$ in
\[
 ds^2=-N(r)^2dt^2+\Psi(r)^4\bigl(dr^2+r^2d\Omega^2\bigr),
\]
the source-free equation is
\[
 \partial_r\!\left(\frac{r^2\Psi^2}{N}A_t'\right)=0,
\]
not $\partial_r(r^2\Psi^2A_t')=0$.  A component along a compact internal
coordinate generically carries additional lapse, radion, and inverse-fiber
metric factors.  Thus the spatial harmonic equation previously used for
$A_\psi$ follows only from a separately stated three-dimensional effective
action or special decoupling assumptions; it is not the generic consequence
of Eq.~\eqref{eq:maxwell}.

Moreover, a nonzero ordinary Maxwell field has nonzero stress-energy and
therefore belongs to the Einstein--Maxwell sector.  It does not generate a
vacuum Schwarzschild geometry.  The exact Schwarzschild lapse is instead a
standard consequence of the static vacuum Einstein equations once the spatial
Schwarzschild branch and boundary data are given.

\textbf{Proved:} the corrected real Abelian current and Maxwell-type equation.
\end{resultbox}
\begin{gapbox}
\textbf{Not proved:} generation of the Schwarzschild lapse by the canonical
UBT connection or by the canonical $\Theta$ Euler--Lagrange equation.
\end{gapbox}
