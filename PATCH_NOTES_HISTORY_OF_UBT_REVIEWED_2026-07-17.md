# Patch Notes — Reviewed History of UBT (2026-07-17)

## Purpose

This history-only patch reviews and strengthens the chronological record of
Unified Biquaternion Theory without changing canonical mathematics or claim
statuses.

## Main changes

- Records the author's handwritten 2013–2015 candidate metric tensors and the
  independent 2013 unified biquaternionic electromagnetic notation.
- Makes clear that this work predates generative-AI assistance.
- Preserves the early superluminal-longitudinal and geometric-invisibility ideas
  as historically important hypotheses while explicitly withholding canonical
  theorem status.
- Adds the relevant modern physics boundary conditions: ordinary Lorentz
  transformations preserve the null cone, standard source-free vacuum plane
  waves are transverse, and transformation-optics cloaking does not require
  biquaternionic spacetime coordinates.
- Retains the author-led AI timeline: AICON Seattle 2025, NDC London 2026, and
  Chinese-developed models from 16 July 2026. Agentic development remains
  human-governed and not fully automated.
- Links the history from `README.md`, records the change in `CHANGELOG.md`, and
  adds regression tests.

## Non-goals

This patch does not validate or reject the unpublished handwritten derivations.
A stronger historical or physical claim requires transcription of those notes,
precise definitions, and a reproducible modern derivation.

No canonical equations, gap statuses, papers, generated PDFs, or research-track
results are changed.
