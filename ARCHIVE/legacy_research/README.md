# Legacy research archive integration

This directory contains only a small, reviewable selection of equation images
from the separate `research-master` archive. It is not a copy of the complete
archive and it is not part of the current canonical derivation.

The complete Google Takeout archives remain external because they are large and
may contain unrelated personal material. Their SHA-256 hashes and the hashes of
the relevant pages are recorded in
`docs/priority_evidence/OCTONION_MULTIVERSE_EVIDENCE.json`.

## Evidence rule

Historical material may support only the mechanism-specific claim named in the
evidence index. It must not be used to backdate the 2026 tetrad, connection, or
GR-dynamical theorems. Files named `transcription_*.tex` are later
human-readable transcriptions and carry no independent timestamp.
